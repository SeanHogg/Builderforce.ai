Sales Discovery Guide

Use this guide to understand the buyer’s current delivery system, quantify the cost of fragmentation, and earn a useful next conversation.

Discovery outcome: a clearly stated business problem, its measurable impact, the people affected, and an agreed next step.

1. Current state

  • How does work move from an idea to a shipped outcome today?
  • Where do humans and AI agents coordinate—or fail to coordinate?
  • Which tools hold planning, delivery, knowledge, and governance?
  • What requires manual reconciliation or status reporting?

2. Impact

  • Where does work stall most often?
  • How much leadership time goes into reconstructing status?
  • What does a delayed release, missed handoff, or failed audit cost?
  • Which decisions lack trustworthy evidence?

3. Desired future

  • If the workflow were unified, what would improve first?
  • Which metric would prove success in 30–90 days?
  • Who needs visibility, control, or approval authority?
  • What would make adoption low-risk?

4. Qualification

  • Problem: Is the pain specific and acknowledged?
  • Priority: Is there a reason to act now?
  • People: Is a decision-maker or champion involved?
  • Process: Is there a real next step and timeline?

Close the conversation

“Based on what you shared, the most useful next step is a focused Builderforce walkthrough around [problem]. Let’s include [stakeholder] and use [metric] as the success criterion.”
