# Stop Emailing References as an Afterthought — Turn Them Into a Job-Winning Asset

## References Are the Most Underused Part of Your Application

By the time an employer asks for references, they're already leaning toward yes — a strong reference either seals that or quietly unravels it. Yet most candidates scramble at the last minute, texting an old manager "can I put you down?" and hoping the phone call goes well.

A better approach: build a standing set of references you've already collected, worded the way you'd want a hiring manager to read them, and ready to share the moment you're asked. Builderforce gives you one place to gather, organize, and present them — so references become a proactive asset instead of a last-minute liability.

## Collect Once, Reuse for Every Application

Instead of re-requesting references for each job, you build a durable library. Add the people who'll speak for you, capture their relationship and title, and store the endorsement text alongside your resume. When a new opportunity comes up, the work is already done — you're not chasing anyone.

Because your references live next to your resumes in one account, you can attach the right people to the right variant: your engineering references for a technical role, your leadership references for a management role.

## You Decide What's Public — Down to the Field

References are sensitive, so Builderforce defaults to private and puts you in control. You choose whether references appear on your public profile at all, and when they do, the public view is deliberately trimmed to non-identifying details — enough to signal credibility without exposing a referrer's personal contact information to the open internet.

That means you can show "a former VP of Engineering endorses this candidate" on your profile while keeping the full contact details reserved for verified employers who genuinely need them. It's social proof without oversharing.

## Pull References Straight Into Your Resume

When you're building or tailoring a resume in the studio, your saved references are one click away — import them as a section without retyping a word. The same source of truth powers your public profile, your references page, and your resume documents, so an update in one place keeps everything consistent.

No more maintaining a separate "references available upon request" line and a scattered list of contacts in your notes app. It's all connected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I put references on my resume?

Not the full contact details — those go to employers who ask. But a signal that you have credible references (with titles and relationships) can strengthen a profile. Builderforce lets you show a trimmed, non-identifying version publicly and keep the private details for verified employers.

### Are my references' contact details exposed publicly?

No. References default to private. If you choose to show them publicly, the public view is limited to non-identifying details. Full contact information is reserved for verified employers, so you get social proof without exposing anyone's personal data.

### Can I use different references for different jobs?

Yes. Build one library and attach the most relevant people to each resume variant — technical references for engineering roles, leadership references for management roles — then import them into the matching resume in a click.
