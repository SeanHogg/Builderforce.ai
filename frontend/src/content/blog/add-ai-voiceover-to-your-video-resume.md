# Add a Studio-Quality AI Voiceover to Your Video Resume — Free, In Your Browser

## You Don't Need a Microphone to Sound Professional

The hardest part of a video resume isn't the words — it's saying them out loud, on camera, without stumbling. A shaky voiceover or a noisy room can undo an otherwise great script. Builderforce removes that barrier entirely: you type your script, pick a voice, and get clean, evenly-paced narration in seconds.

Everything runs inside your browser. There's no upload, no cloud rendering queue, and no per-minute fee — the speech is generated on your own device using WebGPU-accelerated AI voices. That means your script never leaves your machine to be narrated, and you can regenerate a line as many times as you like at zero cost.

## Four Voice Engines, One Click Away

Different moments call for different voices, so Studio ships four narration engines you can switch between per project:

**Instant system voices** — the fastest option, great for a quick draft or a first pass.

**Kokoro-82M** — a warm, natural-sounding neural voice that reads long scripts without the robotic cadence older text-to-speech is known for.

**Piper** — crisp, fast, and lightweight, ideal when you want a clear delivery on a modest device.

**VibeVoice** — the cloning engine, for when you want the narration to sound like *you* (more on that below).

Each voice supports speed control and word-level timing, so the captions on screen light up in sync with the narration automatically.

## Write Once, Narrate Scene by Scene

Studio keeps a separate, editable script for every scene in your video. Rework the hook without touching the closing call-to-action. Tweak one sentence and regenerate just that line — the rest of your voiceover stays exactly as it was.

Because the timing is captured at the word level, your on-screen captions, chapter markers, and scene transitions all stay locked to the narration. Change the script and everything downstream re-times itself, so you're never hand-nudging caption timings after an edit.

## Clone Your Own Voice — Or Earn From It

With the VibeVoice engine you can create a personal voice clone and narrate every project in your own voice, even the scripts you write at 2 a.m. and would never want to record live. Your clone is bound to your account under a perpetual personal license, and our consent terms (ToS §9a) make ownership and permission explicit — you clone your voice, nobody else's.

Creators can go a step further and list a voice in the marketplace. When someone licenses it, you keep the majority of every sale — the split favors the creator, with the small card fee passed to the buyer, not deducted from your earnings. It's a genuinely passive way to monetize a distinctive voice.

## How to Add a Voiceover in Under Two Minutes

1. **Open Studio** and create or open a video resume project.
2. **Write your script** in the scene panel — or let the AI storyboard generate a first draft from your resume.
3. **Pick a voice engine** and adjust the speed to match your energy.
4. **Click generate** — the narration is produced on your device, and captions time themselves to it.
5. **Preview, tweak, and export** in 720p, 1080p, or 4K, then publish straight to YouTube or download for LinkedIn.

No studio, no retakes, no awkward re-recording. Just your story, told in a voice you're proud of.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the AI voiceover really free?

Yes. The neural voices run in your browser using WebGPU, so generating narration costs nothing per minute and works offline once the voice loads. You can regenerate lines as many times as you like without a usage meter.

### Will an AI voiceover sound robotic?

The modern engines (Kokoro-82M, Piper, and VibeVoice) are a generation ahead of classic text-to-speech. They read with natural pacing and inflection, and you can fine-tune speed to match your delivery. For the most authentic result, clone your own voice with VibeVoice.

### Can I make money from a voice clone?

Yes. Creators can list a voice clone in the marketplace and keep the majority of every license sale — the split favors the creator and the card fee is passed to the buyer. Our consent terms require that you only clone a voice you own and have the right to license.
