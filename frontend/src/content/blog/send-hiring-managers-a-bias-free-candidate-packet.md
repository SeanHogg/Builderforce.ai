# How to Send Hiring Managers a Clean, Bias-Free Candidate Packet

## The Problem With Forwarding Resumes

A hiring manager opens your email to find four PDF attachments in four different formats, each laid out differently, one of them a scanned image. They skim the first two, lose the thread, and reply "let's discuss." The momentum you built sourcing those candidates evaporates in the inbox.

The fix is presentation. When your shortlist arrives as a single, consistently formatted packet — every candidate on the same template, easy to compare side by side — the hiring manager can actually make a decision. Builderforce turns a shortlist into exactly that, in a couple of clicks.

## One Packet, Every Candidate, Same Format

Select the candidates you want to present and Builderforce assembles them into a single packet with a shared, professional layout. No wrestling with mismatched resume formats, no reformatting anyone's work history by hand — everyone is rendered through the same clean chrome, so differences in *content* stand out instead of differences in *formatting*.

The packet prints straight to PDF, so you can attach one tidy document or drop it into your ATS. It's the same polished print system that powers the platform's invoices and timesheets, so what the hiring manager sees looks deliberate, not dashed off.

## Anonymize to Reduce Bias — and Get Better Shortlists Reviewed Fairly

Structured, blind review is one of the most effective ways to reduce bias in early screening. Builderforce lets you anonymize the packet with a toggle: names, photos, and other identifying details are hidden, so the hiring manager evaluates skills, experience, and results first.

This protects your candidates and your process. It also tends to produce a fairer, more defensible shortlist — the manager forms an opinion on the substance, and you can reveal identities once the evaluation is grounded in merit. For regulated or DEI-conscious organizations, a repeatable anonymized-review step is a meaningful trust signal.

## How to Build and Send a Packet

1. **Open your hiring workspace** and go to your candidate shortlist.
2. **Select the candidates** you want to present.
3. **Choose your options** — pick a layout and toggle anonymize on if you want a blind review.
4. **Generate the packet** — every candidate is rendered on the same template and combined into one document.
5. **Export to PDF** and share it with the hiring manager or attach it to your ATS.

What used to be an afternoon of copy-paste and formatting is now a two-minute step that makes your whole desk look more professional.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a candidate packet?

It's a single document that presents your shortlisted candidates in one consistent, professional format — instead of forwarding separate, mismatched resume files. Builderforce assembles the packet and exports it to PDF so a hiring manager can compare candidates side by side.

### How does anonymizing a packet help?

Hiding names, photos, and other identifying details lets the hiring manager evaluate skills and experience first, which reduces early-stage bias and produces a fairer, more defensible shortlist. You can reveal identities once the evaluation is grounded in merit.

### Can I put the packet into my ATS?

Yes. The packet exports as a standard PDF, so you can attach it to a candidate record in your ATS, email it to a hiring manager, or archive it with the rest of your submission history.
