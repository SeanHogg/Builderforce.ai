# How to Host a Hiring Event on Builderforce

## Why Run an Event Instead of Posting a Job

A job post is passive — you wait for applicants. An event is active — you create a moment people show up for. Career fairs, virtual info sessions, hiring meetups, and AMAs convert interest into a shortlist faster than a static listing, because attendance is itself a signal of intent.

Events also build employer brand. The candidates who attend your info session arrive warmer to every role you post afterward, because they've already met you. On Builderforce, an event is a first-class object with its own public page, RSVP flow, and management view.

## Step 1: Create the Event

Open [Create Event](/meetings) and fill the essentials: title, description, date and time, format (in-person or virtual), and location or meeting link. Write the description for the candidate — what they'll get out of attending, who should come, what to expect.

A clear, specific title outperforms a clever one. "Backend Engineering Hiring AMA — Tuesday 1pm PT" tells someone exactly whether to show up; "Join us for an exciting evening!" doesn't.

## Step 2: Share the Public Page

Each event gets a public page at its own URL (`/events/<id-or-slug>`) that anyone can view and RSVP to — no account required to see it. Share that link in job posts, on your company page, in outreach, and on social. The page is the canonical home for the event; everything points to it.

Because the page is public and shareable, it doubles as a marketing asset. A well-written event page surfaces in search and gives candidates a clean, branded place to commit.

## Step 3: Manage RSVPs and Run the Day

As people RSVP, manage the event from its [manage view](/meetings) (`/events/<id>/manage`): see who's coming, send updates, and prepare for the headcount. On the day, run the session from the same place — the manage view is your operational home for the event.

After the event, follow up fast. The attendees who showed up are your warmest top-of-funnel; a same-day follow-up while the conversation is fresh converts far better than a generic blast a week later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can candidates RSVP without a Builderforce account?

Each event has a public page at its own URL that anyone can view, so candidates can find and read about the event without signing up. The public page is the shareable home for the event that you point all your promotion toward.

### Where do I manage an event once it's live?

From the event's manage view at /events/<id>/manage. That's where you see RSVPs, send updates, and run the day. The public-facing page at /events/<id-or-slug> is what candidates see.

### What kinds of events work best for hiring?

Anything that creates a moment to show up for: virtual info sessions, hiring AMAs, career fairs, and meetups. They convert interest faster than a static job post because attendance signals intent — and they warm candidates to every role you post afterward.
