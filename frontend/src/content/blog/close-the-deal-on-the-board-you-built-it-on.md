# Close the deal on the board you built it on

A seller's-eye review of the Creation Canvas turned up nine gaps, and they had one thing in common: every single one was in the commercial half. Eighty-plus kinds of object covering *build*, end to end. A demo that could not be handed over. A price that could not be accepted. A follow-up that could not run. A call that could not land on the board.

The capability was mostly there — tokenised share links, an audit log, anonymous rooms, a security evidence register, the ability to actually send mail and post socially. What was missing was **vocabulary**. The commercial half had no objects, so none of it could be put on a board, connected to the work it was selling, or reasoned over by the assistant that already reasons over everything else.

That last one deserves a paragraph. A mutual action plan works because it is jointly owned: dates the buyer agreed to, steps the buyer's own people are named against, a shared view of what is late. A roadmap with the same milestones and no buyer-side owners is a wish list you email at people. The difference is one field, and it is the difference between a plan and a hope.

## Hand a demo to somebody with no account

The most common failure in a build-then-sell motion is that the thing you built lives behind your login. So the demo becomes a screen share, the screen share becomes a recording, and the recording becomes a link nobody watches.

Control is granted live, by the person watching, and never by the token: a link that can escalate its own permissions is a link that escalates after the meeting ends.

## Know what they did with it

Engagement is not a seventh object either. It is activity, recorded under a prospect actor and read back as a rollup: opens, total attention, and per-card hotspots — the parts they kept returning to.

The measurement is deliberately honest. Attention is counted with the tab in front of the reader, and the clock stops when the tab goes to the background. A dwell number that keeps counting behind a backgrounded tab is the fastest way to make every engagement report in a company worthless, because everyone learns the numbers are fiction and stops opening them.

## Where this sits in the method

In the [Idea to Real](/blog/idea-to-real-the-operating-methodology) arc, Market comes after Run — sell, buy, hire, be found. It is deliberately not the first thing built, because a commercial motion in front of a thing that does not work is how software companies waste a year. But it is exactly where a proof stops being an experiment and starts being a business, and a board that cannot carry a quote is a board you have to abandon at that precise moment.

The machinery underneath landed with it: [fixed-price milestones held in escrow](/blog/fixed-price-milestones-and-escrow), [invoices that can be issued, paid and chased](/blog/the-founder-back-office-money-ownership-and-paperwork), and marketplace listings that are checked by actually running them before anything goes on sale.
