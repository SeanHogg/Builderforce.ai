# Run Visual Team Rituals With Humans and AI Agents

Most stand-ups vanish into memory or a transcript. The blockers discussed are separated from the work they block, and follow-ups become a second administrative exercise.

On Creation Canvas, a ritual can be a live object in the same spatial system as the people, agents, projects, and tasks it coordinates.

![Product view of a visual stand-up frame containing human Staff Member and AI Agent participants, current focus, blockers, a Timer, object comments, and three governed follow-up Tasks](/blog/visual-team-rituals-workspace.svg)

## Put the team on the canvas

Staff Member objects carry a person’s role, current focus, and visual accent. Agent objects carry their configured model, instructions, tools, and autonomy. Team and Role objects add organizational context. They can all sit beside the actual Project, Task, Roadmap, or Evaluation under discussion.

A Stand-up object gathers Staff Members and Agents already present in the Session. Each participant contributes a name and current focus. Builderforce connects those participant objects to the ritual with membership relationships rather than copying a roster into disconnected notes.

## Draft locally, start live with durable context

An anonymous local canvas can arrange the team, prepare the Stand-up, and sketch the follow-up system. Starting a collaborative stand-up requires an account because live participants, shared activity, tenant permissions, and follow-up work need durable identity.

In a server Session with a canonical Project, Builderforce can start a project ceremony and attach its resulting identity to the Stand-up object. Without a Project, the same object can remain a prepared ritual frame until the team supplies delivery context.

![Staff Members and Agents join a Stand-up through membership edges; Brain facilitates progress, blockers, and next actions; comments, mentions, drawings, and a timer support the conversation; approved follow-ups become Tasks connected to Project governance](/blog/visual-standup-followup-loop.svg)

## Brain facilitates against visible work

The Stand-up summary tells Brain to ask for progress, blockers, and next actions. Because the ritual shares a canvas with Tasks, diagnostics, prototypes, reports, and Projects, the conversation can point to the objects themselves.

A blocker can cite a Diagnostic. A follow-up can become a Task. A decision can remain a Note or Comment on the affected object. The meeting record is therefore a graph of participation and consequences, not just prose.

## Use the collaboration objects around the ritual

Frames give the session a deliberate meeting area. Drawings support quick explanation. Comments remain attached to objects, with mentions and resolution state. A Timer provides visible focus. Presence shows collaborators without forcing everyone into one viewport, and presentation or follow mode supports a guided review.

The shipped **Impromptu stand-up** pack provides a Stand-up, Staff Member, Delivery Agent, and Follow-up Task as a starting point.

## Close the ritual with accountable work

The useful output of a stand-up is not that everyone spoke. It is that blockers became visible and next actions gained owners, context, and a delivery path.

Creation Canvas preserves that chain:

**participants → current work → blocker evidence → agreed action → governed Task**

Humans and agents can take part in the same ritual because both are represented as accountable members of the work, not as separate chat modes.
