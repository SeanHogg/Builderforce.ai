# Builderforce.ai, Cursor, and Windsurf — Canvas or AI-Native Editor?

Cursor and Windsurf are evaluated primarily as AI-native engineering environments. Builderforce.ai starts earlier: people and agents shape connected artifacts on a creative canvas, then move supported work into projects, workflows, and engineering surfaces.

## Run a representative test

Ask each product to carry one feature from discovery through a reviewed implementation. Measure:

- quality and speed of code editing;
- participation by product, design, and engineering;
- preservation of decisions and related artifacts;
- approval and recovery behavior;
- execution and data boundaries;
- current model, integration, and plan requirements.

BuilderForce Agents can be deployed separately on private infrastructure, while the Builderforce.ai workspace, collaboration, publishing, and connected-service features have distinct hosted boundaries. Do not treat “self-hosted runtime” as meaning that every platform feature is local or air-gapped.

Choose an AI-native editor when editor ergonomics are the main job. Choose a connected canvas when the larger problem is keeping ideas, evidence, people, agents, and delivery work together. Some teams will use both.
