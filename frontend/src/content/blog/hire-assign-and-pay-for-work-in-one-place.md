# Hire, Assign, Track, and Pay for Work — All in One Place

## The Handoff Problem

You find someone great, agree on the work, and then the process fragments: the brief lives in a chat thread, the progress lives in their head, the invoice arrives by email, and payment happens in yet another tool. Every seam is a place for work to stall or a payment to be forgotten.

Builderforce closes that loop. Hiring, assigning, tracking, approving, and paying all happen on one connected surface — so nothing depends on remembering to move information between apps. The person doing the work and the person paying for it are looking at the same board.

## From "You're Hired" to "You're Paid" Without Leaving the App

Once someone is engaged, you assign them an activity — a defined piece of paid work with clear expectations. They see it in their own view, do the work, and submit it for review. You approve it. Payment accrues automatically against that approved work. No separate invoice to chase, no reconciling a timesheet against a to-do list.

Because each step is a real status the whole team can see, there's never ambiguity about whether something is in progress, waiting on you, or done and owed. The board *is* the source of truth.

## Everyone Sees Their Own Slice

The person you've hired gets a focused view of exactly what's assigned to them and what they've submitted — no clutter, no access to things that aren't theirs. You get the manager's view: what's outstanding, what's waiting on your approval, and what's accrued to pay.

That clean separation matters. Contributors aren't overwhelmed by the whole operation, and you aren't fielding "what should I work on?" messages. Assign it once and it shows up where it needs to.

## Approvals You Can Stand Behind

Money should move on approved work, not on trust alone. Builderforce keeps a clear record: the activity was assigned, submitted, reviewed, and approved — and the payout is tied to that trail. If a question ever comes up about what was paid and why, the answer is right there in the activity's history.

The result is a workflow that scales past one or two contractors without turning into a spreadsheet you dread opening. You spend your time deciding what needs doing, not administering how it got tracked and paid.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does paying for work happen on Builderforce?

When you approve a submitted activity, payment accrues against that approved work automatically and is recorded in the payout ledger. You're paying on reviewed-and-approved work with a clear trail, not chasing separate invoices.

### Does the person I hire see everything?

No. Contributors get a focused view of only the activities assigned to them and what they've submitted. Managers get the full board — outstanding work, items waiting on approval, and what's accrued to pay.

### Why keep hiring, tracking, and payment in one tool?

Every handoff between separate apps is a place for work to stall or a payment to slip. Running the whole loop — assign, track, submit, approve, pay — on one board means the whole team shares a single source of truth and nothing depends on manually moving data between tools.
