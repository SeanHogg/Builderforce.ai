# Earn Verifiable Certificates and Actually Level Up — Not Just Collect PDFs

## A Certificate Nobody Can Verify Is Just a Picture

You finish a course, download a PDF, and add it to your profile — but when an employer clicks it, there's no way to confirm it's real. That's the quiet weakness of most online learning: the credential is unverifiable, so it carries almost no weight.

Builderforce issues certificates that an employer can actually check. Each one is cryptographically signed, so anyone you share it with can confirm it was issued to you, for that course, on that date — and that it hasn't been altered. Your effort turns into a credential that holds up under scrutiny.

## Start From a Goal, Not a Random Course

Learning sticks when it's pointed at something. Instead of picking courses at random, you tell Builderforce the goal — "become a front-end engineer," "move into people management" — and it assembles a structured path of the right courses in the right order.

Behind the scenes this is a real learning-path engine, not a static playlist: prerequisites are respected, and your progress unlocks the next step. You always know what to do next and why it matters for where you're trying to go.

## Momentum, Built In

The reason most people abandon online courses isn't difficulty — it's losing momentum. Builderforce Learning is designed to keep you moving. You earn points for progress, build a daily streak, and unlock badges as you hit milestones. A leaderboard lets you measure your pace against your cohort or the whole community when you want a push.

Your learner dashboard pulls it all into one view: what you're enrolled in, what's due, how far along you are, your streak, your points, and the certificates you've already earned. Open it and you immediately know your next move.

## An AI Coach That Knows What You're Studying

Stuck on a concept? The built-in AI coach is aware of the exact course and lesson you're on, so you can ask a question in plain language and get an answer grounded in what you're actually learning — no copying context into a separate chatbot.

It's the difference between a generic search and a tutor who's been reading over your shoulder. Ask it to re-explain a topic, quiz you, or connect a lesson to your career goal.

## For Employers and Educators, Too

The same system powers structured training for teams and classrooms. Employers can author their own courses and learning paths, assign them, and track completion; educators can run cohorts with a gradebook that keeps every learner's assignments aligned. Certificates issued through that flow carry the same verifiable signature — so internal training produces credentials that mean something outside the company, too.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is a Builderforce certificate verifiable?

Each certificate is cryptographically signed when issued. Anyone you share it with can confirm it was genuinely issued to you, for that course, on that date, and that it hasn't been tampered with — unlike a plain PDF that anyone could edit.

### Do I have to pick courses one at a time?

No. Tell Builderforce your goal and it assembles a structured learning path — the right courses in the right order, with prerequisites respected. You can still enroll in individual courses, but the path keeps you pointed at an outcome.

### Can my company create its own courses?

Yes. Employers and educators can author courses and learning paths, assign them, run cohorts with a gradebook, and track completion. Certificates from those courses carry the same verifiable signature as public ones.
