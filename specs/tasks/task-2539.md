# Meeting Notes Marketplace Agent

## Problem & Goal

Advisors currently lack a first-class, marketplace-discoverable Meeting Notes agent. Note-taking is either manual, bolted onto the advisory turn path, or inconsistent across dogfood. We need a published marketplace `agent` listing that wraps `runAgentTurn` with a silent notetaker persona, scoped to `kind=advisory`, and enabled by default for dogfood advisors so every advisory session produces notes without extra setup.

**Goal:** Ship Meeting Notes as a marketplace `agent` that silently captures advisory turns via `runAgentTurn`, is default-on for dogfood advisors, and is discoverable/toggleable like any other listing.

## Target users / ICP roles

- **Dogfood advisors** — primary; default-on, silent capture during advisory sessions
- **Advisor operators / marketplace admins** — publish, configure, and roll out the listing
- **Downstream agents** — consume the listing contract (`agent` type, `runAgentTurn` wrap, `kind=advisory`) without custom wiring
- **Advisees** — not a control-surface user; notes are a byproduct of the advisory session

## Scope

- Publish Meeting Notes as a marketplace listing with type `agent`
- Wrap existing `runAgentTurn` (no parallel turn runtime)
- Silent notetaker persona only; no participant-facing chatter
- Restrict invocation to sessions with `kind=advisory`
- Default-on for dogfood advisors; remaining audiences follow normal marketplace enablement
- Listing metadata, enablement, and dogfood default so downstream agents share one contract

## Functional requirements

1. **Listing type:** Marketplace entry is `agent` (not skill, workflow, or tool).
2. **Runtime wrap:** Each eligible turn is handled by `runAgentTurn`; the listing does not introduce a second execution path.
3. **Persona:** Silent notetaker — produces notes from the turn; does not speak, interrupt, or send participant-visible messages.
4. **Kind gate:** Runs only when session/turn `kind=advisory`; other kinds are no-ops and must not invoke the notetaker.
5. **Dogfood default:** For dogfood advisors, Meeting Notes is on without opt-in. They may disable it via the same marketplace controls as other agents.
6. **Discoverability:** Listing is visible in the marketplace with name, description, and `agent` type consistent with other listings.
7. **Idempotent enablement:** Re-enabling or duplicate default-on application does not double-subscribe or duplicate notes.
8. **Failure isolation:** `runAgentTurn` or notetaker failures must not fail the advisory turn for participants.

## Acceptance criteria

- Marketplace shows a Meeting Notes listing with type `agent`.
- Enabling the listing binds it to `runAgentTurn` with the silent notetaker persona.
- On `kind=advisory`, notes are produced from the turn; the notetaker emits no participant-visible messages.
- On any other `kind`, the agent does not run and no notes are written.
- New and existing dogfood advisors have Meeting Notes on by default after publish.
- A dogfood advisor can turn the listing off and it stays off across sessions until turned back on.
- Non-dogfood users do not get default-on unless separately enabled.
- Advisory session success is independent of notetaker success or failure.
- Downstream agents can resolve and invoke this listing from the shared marketplace contract with no one-off flags.

## Out of scope

- Non-advisory kinds (coaching, support, internal standups, etc.)
- Participant-facing recap, chat, or “notes bot” messages
- New note storage, export, or CRM sync beyond what `runAgentTurn` already provides
- Custom model, prompt pipeline, or runtime besides wrapping `runAgentTurn`
- Default-on for non-dogfood advisors or other marketplace audiences
- Multi-agent orchestration, human editor UX, or note-quality scoring
- Pricing, public marketplace launch, or non-dogfood GA packaging
