# PRD: Journey Launch Checklist

**Status:** WIP  
**Owner:** Product Architecture  
**Related:** Growth `sendReadiness` (suppression, unsubscribe, transport) — **do not duplicate**

## Problem & Goal

Journeys fail late because brand, consent, sender, offer, and copy are treated as informal notes rather than required launch gates. Growth already owns **send-time** readiness (`sendReadiness`: suppression, unsubscribe, transport). That leaves a gap: **journey-time** completeness for creative, targeting, and channel identity.

**Goal:** A single, machine-readable Journey Checklist that every journey must satisfy before it can leave draft (and that downstream agents can evaluate the same way). Items are: brand (voice + doNotSay), consent audience, sender/account for the chosen channel, offer, copy. Distinct from, and complementary to, Growth `sendReadiness`.

Success looks like: a journey cannot be marked launch-ready until all five items pass; failures are item-specific and actionable; Growth send checks remain the source of truth for suppression / unsubscribe / transport.

## Target users / ICP roles

- **Journey builder** (lifecycle / CRM marketer): configures the path and needs a clear “what’s missing” list, not a send-infra dump.
- **Brand / legal reviewer:** owns voice and doNotSay; must see those as first-class gates, not buried in copy review.
- **Channel ops:** owns sender/account mapping per channel (email from-address/domain, SMS short/long code, push app, etc.).
- **Downstream agents** (implementation, QA, Growth integration): consume the same checklist schema; must never reimplement `sendReadiness`.

## Scope

In scope for this WIP:

1. **Checklist model** attached to a Journey (not a one-off campaign send, not a Growth send job).
2. **Five items only:** brand (voice + doNotSay), consent audience, sender/account for chosen channel, offer, copy.
3. **Per-channel evaluation** where the journey has a chosen channel (sender/account and copy are channel-aware).
4. **Pass / fail / incomplete** states with human-readable reasons and deep links to the owning record.
5. **Launch gate:** journey cannot transition to launch-ready / scheduled / live while any required item is fail or incomplete.
6. **Explicit non-overlap contract** with Growth `sendReadiness`.

Out of scope is listed at the end.

## Functional requirements

### FR-1 — Checklist is a first-class Journey resource

- Every Journey has exactly one checklist instance.
- Checklist is derived from Journey configuration + linked records; it is not a freeform to-do list.
- Schema is stable for agents: `itemId`, `status` (`pass` | `fail` | `incomplete`), `reason`, `ownerType`, `recordId`, `channel` (nullable).

### FR-2 — Brand: voice + doNotSay

- **Pass** when the Journey is linked to a Brand that has:
  - a non-empty **voice** definition (tone, personality, writing constraints used by copy generation/review), and
  - a **doNotSay** list (phrases, claims, competitor names, legal-sensitive language) that is non-empty **or** explicitly marked “none required” by a brand owner.
- **Incomplete** if no Brand is linked.
- **Fail** if Brand is linked but voice is empty, or doNotSay is neither populated nor explicitly waived.
- Voice and doNotSay are evaluated as **two sub-checks under one item** (`brand`). Both must pass for the item to pass.
- Checklist does not invent brand copy; it only verifies presence and link.

### FR-3 — Consent audience

- **Pass** when the Journey has a consent audience: a named audience whose membership is defined by an explicit consent basis appropriate to the Journey’s purpose and channel (e.g. marketing opt-in for promotional email/SMS).
- Audience must be resolvable (exists, not deleted, not a raw unconsented list).
- **Incomplete** if no audience is attached.
- **Fail** if audience is attached but has no consent basis, consent basis is incompatible with the chosen channel, or the audience is marked non-sendable for consent reasons.
- This item is **consent and audience selection**, not suppression, unsubscribe processing, or transport health (those stay in Growth `sendReadiness`).

### FR-4 — Sender / account for chosen channel

- Journey must have a **chosen channel** (email, SMS, push, or whatever the Journey runtime supports).
- **Pass** when that channel has a linked **sender/account** that is active and eligible for that channel (verified sending identity, not a placeholder).
- Multi-step / multi-channel journeys: **each chosen channel** must have its own sender/account; the item fails if any chosen channel is missing one.
- **Incomplete** if channel is unset or sender/account is unset.
- **Fail** if sender/account is linked but inactive, unverified, wrong channel type, or not authorized for this workspace.
- Does **not** include deliverability, IP warmup, bounce rates, or MTA/provider health (`sendReadiness.transport`).

### FR-5 — Offer

- **Pass** when the Journey is linked to an Offer with: identifier, customer-facing value proposition, and any required legal/promo constraints the copy item will need (expiry, eligibility, code).
- **Incomplete** if no Offer is linked and the Journey type requires one (promotional / conversion journeys).
- **Fail** if Offer is linked but missing required fields, expired relative to the Journey’s intended send window, or marked inactive.
- Operational / transactional journey types may mark offer as **not applicable**; that is a pass with `reason = not_applicable`, not a skip of the item.

### FR-6 — Copy

- **Pass** when every message step on a chosen channel has copy that is:
  - present (non-empty body; subject/preview/headline where the channel requires it),
  - bound to the Journey’s Brand (so voice + doNotSay can be applied),
  - bound to the Offer when the offer item is applicable.
- **Incomplete** if any required message slot is empty.
- **Fail** if copy exists but is for the wrong channel, missing required channel fields, or not linked to Brand/Offer when those items are required.
- Copy completeness ≠ brand compliance scoring. This item is **presence and binding**. Optional later: a brand-lint warning that does not block unless product policy says so; default for WIP is **block on missing copy, warn on voice/doNotSay lint**.

### FR-7 — Distinct from Growth `sendReadiness`

- Journey Checklist **must not** evaluate or display: suppression lists, unsubscribe state, RFC-compliant unsubscribe headers, bounce/complaint handling, or transport/provider readiness.
- UI and API copy must name this surface **Journey checklist** (or equivalent), never `sendReadiness`.
- Launch-ready on the Journey side means checklist pass **only**. Actual send still requires Growth `sendReadiness` at send time. Both gates are required; neither implies the other.
- Shared identifiers (journeyId, audienceId, channel, senderId) may be passed to Growth; checklist logic must not call into or fork `sendReadiness` internals.

### FR-8 — Gate behavior

- Default: all five items are **required** unless an item is explicitly `not_applicable` (offer on transactional journeys).
- Status rollup: Journey `checklistStatus = pass` iff every required item is `pass`.
- Transitions to scheduled / live / launch-ready are rejected with the list of failing/incomplete items.
- Edits to Brand, audience, sender, offer, or copy recompute the checklist immediately.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Creating a Journey yields a checklist with five items in `incomplete` (or offer `not_applicable` when the type warrants it).
2. Linking a Brand with voice **and** doNotSay (or an explicit doNotSay waiver) moves `brand` to `pass`; removing voice or doNotSay without waiver moves it to `fail`.
3. Attaching a consent-qualified audience moves `consentAudience` to `pass`; a list with no consent basis is `fail`.
4. Selecting email without a verified from-identity leaves `senderAccount` `incomplete`/`fail`; attaching a valid identity for that channel is `pass`. A second channel without its own sender keeps the item `fail`.
5. Promotional journeys cannot pass `offer` without an active, complete Offer; transactional journeys can pass with `not_applicable`.
6. A message step with empty body keeps `copy` `incomplete`; filling required channel fields and binding Brand (and Offer if applicable) yields `pass`.
7. API/UI never surfaces suppression, unsubscribe, or transport under this checklist. Those remain Growth `sendReadiness` only.
8. Attempting to schedule/launch with any required item not `pass` is rejected; the response lists item ids and reasons.
9. Two agents evaluating the same Journey produce the same checklist statuses from the same records.
10. Passing this checklist does not mark a send job send-ready in Growth; failing `sendReadiness` does not fail this checklist unless a shared record (e.g. deleted audience) independently fails an item here.

## Out of scope

- Growth `sendReadiness`: suppression, unsubscribe, transport, ISP reputation, seed tests, throttling.
- Full brand-compliance or legal auto-redline of copy (beyond presence of voice + doNotSay and optional lint warning).
- Consent capture UX, preference-center design, or lawful-basis engines beyond “audience has a consent basis compatible with channel.”
- Sender onboarding / domain authentication flows (checklist only consumes an already-eligible sender/account).
- Offer merchandising, inventory, or coupon issuance.
- Copy generation, translation, or A/B experiment setup (checklist only requires bound copy slots).
- Journey orchestration runtime, entry criteria, wait steps, or analytics.
- Cross-product campaign calendar, approvals workflow (beyond the mechanical launch gate), or billing.
