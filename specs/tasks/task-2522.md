- `/marketplace?category=advisors` is a **filter**, not `/advisors` and not a new `MARKETPLACE_LISTING_KINDS` value.
- Do not add `advisory` to `PEOPLE_OBJECT_KINDS`.
- A0 bind: `booking_hosts.host_ref = talent userId` (never `freelancer_profiles.booking_service_id`).

- No SCORE / SBA branding. No new video, notes schema, CMS/LMS, or payout rail.
- Do not clobber existing `BookAdvisorPanel` / `talent.book*` keys.
