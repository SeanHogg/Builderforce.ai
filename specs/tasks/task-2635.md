# PRD: Connect Success Never Sends or Spends

**Status:** WIP  
**Owner:** Product Architecture  
**Priority:** P0 (safety invariant)

## Problem & Goal

Users treat **Connect** as an authorization or linking step. In practice, connect success has been observed (or is at risk) of chaining into transfer, payment, or spend side effects—either automatically, via pre-authorized payloads, or through reused signing/session primitives. That violates user intent, creates irreversible fund movement, and destroys trust.

**Goal:** Make it an inviolable product rule that a successful Connect completes linking/session establishment only. Connect success must never initiate, queue, sign, broadcast, or otherwise cause a send or spend. Any movement of value requires a separate, explicit user action after connect has already succeeded.

## Target users / ICP roles

- End users connecting an account, wallet, or payment method who expect no money movement from Connect itself.
- Power users / treasury operators connecting multiple accounts who must trust Connect is non-custodial of spend authority until they later confirm a payment.
- Support, compliance, and risk teams who need a clear invariant when investigating unexpected transfers.
- Integrators and partner surfaces that embed Connect and must not inherit accidental spend behavior.

## Scope

In scope:

- The Connect happy path (initiate → authenticate/approve → success → linked session).
- Connect error, cancel, timeout, and retry paths (must also not send or spend).
- Post-connect session state: tokens, grants, capabilities, and UI that appear immediately after success.
- Any automatic follow-on jobs, webhooks, SDK callbacks, or deeplinks fired because Connect succeeded.
- Pre-filled, batched, or “one-click” payloads that might ride along with the connect request.
- Telemetry, receipts, and user-visible copy that would imply a send/spend occurred as part of Connect.

## Functional requirements

1. **Connect is link-only.** On success, the system may persist identity, account reference, read capabilities, and a non-spend session. It must not create a transfer, payment, withdrawal, on-chain send, card charge, or equivalent spend.
2. **No bundled spend authorization.** The Connect request, approval UI, and granted scopes must not include spend, transfer, or unlimited-sign permissions required to move value. Spend authority is obtained only in a later, dedicated flow.
3. **No automatic follow-on execution.** Connect success must not enqueue or trigger send/spend workers, “complete setup by paying,” airdrop claims that move user funds, gas sponsorship that debit the user, or partner callbacks that execute a payment.
4. **Separate confirmation for value movement.** Any send or spend requires its own explicit user confirmation after Connect has already reached success (or independently of Connect). Reusing the Connect signature, biometric, or session nonce as a spend authorization is forbidden.
5. **Idempotent and side-effect-free retries.** Repeating Connect, reconnecting, or handling duplicate success events must not produce a send or spend.
6. **Honest UI and API.** Success screens, emails, webhooks, and API responses for Connect must describe linking/session only. They must not show amounts sent, “payment complete,” or spend receipts.
7. **Fail closed.** If Connect cannot complete without attaching a spend payload, pre-signed transfer, or spend-capable grant, Connect must fail. It must not succeed “and also send.”
8. **Auditability.** Every Connect success is logged as a non-financial linking event. Send/spend events are a distinct audit type and must never share the Connect success event id as their cause.

## Acceptance criteria

- Completing Connect with a funded account leaves balances, allowances, and on-chain/off-chain ledgers unchanged (except non-value metadata such as session records).
- Network, ledger, and processor traces during Connect show no transfer, charge, withdrawal, or broadcast spend.
- Connect success webhooks/SDK events contain no payment/transfer identifiers and do not invoke send/spend endpoints.
- Granted Connect scopes/capabilities exclude send, spend, transfer, and unbounded signing; attempting to attach them causes Connect to fail before success.
- Cancel, timeout, network drop, and double-submit of Connect produce zero send/spend.
- A user can Connect successfully and then leave the product without any delayed send/spend firing.
- QA matrix (first connect, reconnect, expired session, multiple accounts, zero-balance and funded accounts) shows zero value movement attributable to Connect success.
- Support can distinguish Connect-success audit records from send/spend records; no Connect-success record is the parent of a funds-out record.

## Out of scope

- Design of the later, explicit send/spend/confirm-payment flows (except that they must not be triggered by Connect success).
- Custody model, yield, or investing features unrelated to the Connect path.
- Marketing, onboarding copy, or growth experiments that are not part of Connect execution.
- Third-party scams outside the product’s Connect implementation (in-product Connect must still fail closed).
- Historical backfill of past incidents; this PRD governs current and future Connect behavior.
