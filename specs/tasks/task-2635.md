# PRD: Connect success never sends or spends

## Problem & Goal

Connect is an account- or wallet-linking flow. A successful Connect must only establish a trusted session or linked identity. It must not move value.

Today that invariant is not an explicit product rule. Downstream implementations can treat Connect success as a trigger for transfers, payments, token approvals, or other spend. That creates unexpected fund movement, support load, and loss of user trust.

**Goal:** Make it a hard, testable product invariant that Connect success never sends and never spends. Connect may authenticate, persist a link, and show confirmation. It must not create, sign, broadcast, queue, or execute any value-moving operation.

## Target users / ICP roles

- End users completing Connect (retail and power users)
- Support agents triaging “I connected and money moved”
- Product, payments, and wallet engineers implementing Connect
- Security, fraud, and compliance reviewers of the Connect path

## Scope

In scope:

- Happy-path Connect success (new link and re-link)
- Post-success side effects in client, backend, and any payment/wallet adapter
- Any automatic follow-on that could send, spend, transfer, charge, tip, donate, or approve spend
- User-visible confirmation that Connect succeeded without a send or spend
- Telemetry and audit evidence that success did not move value

Out of scope is listed below.

## Functional requirements

1. **Connect success is link-only.** On success the product may create or refresh a session, store a linked account/wallet identifier, and show a success state. It must not create a payment, transfer, withdrawal, on-chain send, or equivalent.

2. **No implicit spend.** Success must not debit balance, capture a card, reserve funds, burn credits, spend gas as a value transfer, or submit a token allowance / unlimited approval as part of Connect.

3. **No chained send.** Success handlers, webhooks, jobs, and client callbacks must not enqueue or fire send/spend. A later send requires a separate, explicit user intent after Connect has already completed.

4. **Signatures stay non-spending.** If Connect uses a signature or challenge, it must be authentication-only (login/link proof). It must not be a transaction, typed-data spend, permit, or approval that can move or unlock funds.

5. **Idempotent success.** Retrying or replaying Connect success (double callback, refresh, deep link reopen) must not send or spend.

6. **Failure isolation.** Connect errors, timeouts, and partial success must not fall through into a send/spend path.

7. **User confirmation.** The success screen states that the account/wallet is connected and that no funds were sent or spent.

8. **Auditability.** Each Connect success writes an audit record with outcome `connected`, amount `0`, and no payment/tx id. Any send/spend elsewhere must not share the Connect success event as its cause.

## Acceptance criteria

- Completing Connect (first time and repeat) results in a linked session and success UI, with balance unchanged and no payment/transfer/tx created.
- Network, ledger, and processor logs for the Connect success window show zero send, capture, withdrawal, broadcast, or spend-approval.
- Automated tests cover: success, retry/replay, webhook duplicate, client resume, and offline-then-sync; none produce a send or spend.
- A signature/challenge used in Connect cannot be executed as a transfer or allowance; attempting to treat it as one is rejected.
- QA and security review sign off that Connect success handlers contain no payment, wallet-send, or spend-approval calls.
- Support can confirm from audit logs that a given Connect success moved no value.
- Feature flags or experiments on Connect cannot enable an automatic send/spend on success.

## Out of scope

- The separate, explicit Send / Spend / Pay flows after a user is already connected
- Funding, deposits, or receive addresses shown after Connect
- Disconnect, revoke, and re-auth UX except where they must also avoid send/spend
- Pricing, rewards, or marketing copy unrelated to the no-send invariant
- New payment methods, chains, or processors beyond ensuring Connect success still never sends or spends on them
- Recovery of historical incidents where Connect already moved funds (ops runbook, not this change)

## Requirements

Connect, for this ticket, is the C1 guided-campaign **account-linking** step and the existing link surfaces it uses. It is not Send, not Spend, and not C3 confirm.

**In-scope Connect surfaces (link-only):**

- C1 `guidedCampaignRun` connect step (parent #2629; adapter remains `frontend/src/lib/canvasMarketing.ts`).
- Mailbox OAuth: `buildProviderConsentUrl` / `completeProviderOAuthCallback` / `saveMailboxConnection` on `/api/mailbox/*` (`mailboxRoutes.ts`). Connecting is DEVELOPER-level.
- Social and ads account link: connector credential store `/api/connectors` (`createConnection`). Connecting an ad or social account is **not** `/api/ads` or `/api/social` (`adsRoutes.ts` and `socialRoutes.ts` already document this split).
- Sender identity: `createSender` / `verifySender` on `/api/marketing/*`.
- Connectable catalog: `GET /api/integrations/connectable` (`connectableCatalog.ts`) — describe and store credentials; do not fire a send.

**Out-of-scope for Connect (value-moving; remain on their own tickets):**

- Email/SMS send: `startCampaign`, `runCampaignBatch`, `sendFromMailbox`, `sendMailboxAutomationExecution`; Brain `campaign.send` / `campaign.schedule`.
- Social publish: `POST /api/social/publish`, `POST /api/social/campaigns/:id/publish` (`publishSocialPost`, `runSocialCampaignBatch`); Brain `social_campaign.publish`.
- Ads spend: `POST /api/ads/campaigns` (`createAdCampaign` with `launch`), `PATCH` that activates or re-budgets (`updateAdCampaign`); Brain ads launch.
- Mailbox blast opt-in: `setMailboxSending({ allowSending: true })` — MANAGER-gated and **not** part of OAuth success.
- C1 `maySend` five-way conjunction (#2633) and C3 `humanConfirmed` (#2638).
- Login OAuth (`oauthRoutes.ts`) except that it also must not send or spend.
- No wallet / on-chain Connect surface exists in this repo; this ticket does not invent one.

**Assumptions:** C1 contract (`guidedCampaignRun`) lands on #2629. `maySend` stays the #2633 conjunction. C3 is the only writer of `humanConfirmed=true`. Connect success may flip a checklist “account connected” item; it must not flip `maySend` or `humanConfirmed`.

### R-1 — Connect success is link-only

On first link and re-link, Connect MAY create or refresh a session, persist a mailbox / connector / sender identifier, and show a success state. It MUST NOT create a payment, transfer, withdrawal, campaign send, social publish, ads launch, or equivalent. Completing Connect MUST leave `maySend` false unless every #2633 conjunct is already independently true (Connect itself supplies none of `humanConfirmed`, `checklist.ready`, or `sendReadiness.ready`).

### R-2 — No implicit spend

Connect success MUST NOT debit balance, capture a card, reserve funds, burn credits, submit a token allowance, or spend ads budget. Ads connect stores a connector credential only. `POST /api/ads/campaigns` and any `launch: true` / status=`active` patch are forbidden in Connect success handlers, OAuth callbacks, and C1 connect-step completion.

### R-3 — Mailbox OAuth callback never sends

`completeProviderOAuthCallback` / `saveMailboxConnection` MUST persist the grant and return a connected mailbox. They MUST NOT call `sendFromMailbox`, `startCampaign`, `runCampaignBatch`, or `sendMailboxAutomationExecution`. New connections MUST persist with `allowSending` false (or unset). Enabling send is a separate MANAGER action via `setMailboxSending`.

### R-4 — Connector create never publishes or launches

`createConnection` (and OAuth-complete equivalents) for social or ads MUST store credentials and return a connected account. They MUST NOT call `publishSocialPost`, `runSocialCampaignBatch`, `createAdCampaign`, or `updateAdCampaign`. `POST /api/social/campaigns` MAY draft (no publish) only if the operator explicitly created a draft — not as a Connect side effect.

### R-5 — Sender verify never starts a campaign

`createSender` / `verifySender` MAY mark a sender identity verified. They MUST NOT call `startCampaign` or `runCampaignBatch`. A verified sender is a C1 checklist input, not a send.

### R-6 — No chained send

Success handlers, provider webhooks (mailbox Pub/Sub / Graph push), jobs, client callbacks, and C1 connect-step completion MUST NOT enqueue or fire send/spend. `handleGmailPush` / `handleGraphPush` MAY ingest mail for the inbox/watch; they MUST NOT start a campaign. A later send requires a separate, explicit user intent **after** Connect has already completed, gated by #2633 `maySend` and #2638 C3 confirm.

### R-7 — Signatures stay non-spending

Mailbox / connector OAuth `state` (HMAC-signed, same primitive as calendar) is authentication-only. It MUST NOT be treated as a transaction, typed-data spend, permit, allowance, or campaign-send authorization. Attempting to execute Connect `state` or an OAuth code as a send/spend MUST be rejected.

### R-8 — Idempotent success

Retrying or replaying Connect success (double OAuth callback, refresh, deep-link reopen, duplicate webhook, client resume, offline-then-sync) MUST NOT send or spend. Replay MAY no-op or refresh the same linked identity. It MUST NOT increment send counters, create a second campaign batch, publish, or launch ads.

### R-9 — Failure isolation

Connect errors, timeouts, partial success (user granted, persist failed), and provider `state` mismatch MUST NOT fall through into a send/spend path. A failed Connect MUST NOT set `allowSending`, MUST NOT set `humanConfirmed`, and MUST NOT call campaign/social/ads write endpoints.

### R-10 — User confirmation

The Connect success UI (C1 connect step and mailbox/social/ads connect panels) MUST state that the account is connected and that no message was sent and no ads budget was spent. It MUST NOT show send/publish/launch as having occurred. Copy MAY point to the separate confirm-and-send step (C3).

### R-11 — Auditability

Each Connect success MUST write an audit record with outcome `connected`, amount `0`, and no payment / campaign-send / publish / ads-campaign id as cause. Any later send/spend MUST use its own cause (C3 confirm / `maySend` / `startCampaign` / publish / ads launch). Support MUST be able to confirm from that record that a given Connect moved no value.

### R-12 — Feature flags cannot auto-send

Experiments or flags on Connect MUST NOT enable automatic send, publish, or ads launch on success. A flag MAY hide Connect, change copy, or gate which providers appear. A flag that would call `startCampaign`, `publishSocialPost`, or `createAdCampaign({ launch: true })` from a Connect path is a defect.

### R-13 — Distinct from `maySend` and C3

Connect success MUST NOT set `humanConfirmed=true` (C3 #2638 is the only writer). Connect success MUST NOT by itself satisfy #2633 `maySend`. Cloud agents remain draft-only (epic #2547): a Connect performed during an agent turn MUST NOT send, publish, or spend. Anonymous canvas MAY author; it MUST NEVER Connect-then-send.

### R-14 — Tests the invariant

Automated tests MUST cover: first-time Connect, re-link, OAuth callback retry/replay, webhook duplicate, client resume, offline-then-sync, and failed/partial Connect. None of those cases MAY produce `startCampaign`, `runCampaignBatch`, `sendFromMailbox`, `publishSocialPost`, `runSocialCampaignBatch`, or `createAdCampaign`/`updateAdCampaign` spend. A signature/`state` treated as a transfer or send MUST be rejected. QA/security review of Connect handlers MUST show no payment, wallet-send, campaign-send, publish, or spend-approval calls.
