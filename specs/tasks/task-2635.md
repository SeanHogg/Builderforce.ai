# PRD: Connect success never sends or spends

## Problem & Goal

Connect is an account- or wallet-linking flow. A successful Connect must only establish a trusted session or linked identity. It must not move value.

Today that invariant is not an explicit product rule. Downstream implementations can treat Connect success as a trigger for transfers, payments, token approvals, or other spend. That creates unexpected fund movement, support load, and loss of user trust.

**Goal:** Make it a hard, testable product invariant that Connect success never sends and never spends. Connect may authenticate, persist a link, and show confirmation. It must not create, sign, broadcast, queue, or execute any value-moving operation.

## Target users / ICP roles

- End users completing Connect (retail and power users)
- Support agents triaging “I connected and money moved”
- Product, payments, and wallet engineers implementing Connect
- Security, fraud, and compliance reviewers of the Connect path

## Scope

In scope:

- Happy-path Connect success (new link and re-link)
- Post-success side effects in client, backend, and any payment/wallet adapter
- Any automatic follow-on that could send, spend, transfer, charge, tip, donate, or approve spend
- User-visible confirmation that Connect succeeded without a send or spend
- Telemetry and audit evidence that success did not move value

Out of scope is listed below.

## Functional requirements

1. **Connect success is link-only.** On success the product may create or refresh a session, store a linked account/wallet identifier, and show a success state. It must not create a payment, transfer, withdrawal, on-chain send, or equivalent.

2. **No implicit spend.** Success must not debit balance, capture a card, reserve funds, burn credits, spend gas as a value transfer, or submit a token allowance / unlimited approval as part of Connect.

3. **No chained send.** Success handlers, webhooks, jobs, and client callbacks must not enqueue or fire send/spend. A later send requires a separate, explicit user intent after Connect has already completed.

4. **Signatures stay non-spending.** If Connect uses a signature or challenge, it must be authentication-only (login/link proof). It must not be a transaction, typed-data spend, permit, or approval that can move or unlock funds.

5. **Idempotent success.** Retrying or replaying Connect success (double callback, refresh, deep link reopen) must not send or spend.

6. **Failure isolation.** Connect errors, timeouts, and partial success must not fall through into a send/spend path.

7. **User confirmation.** The success screen states that the account/wallet is connected and that no funds were sent or spent.

8. **Auditability.** Each Connect success writes an audit record with outcome `connected`, amount `0`, and no payment/tx id. Any send/spend elsewhere must not share the Connect success event as its cause.

## Acceptance criteria

- Completing Connect (first time and repeat) results in a linked session and success UI, with balance unchanged and no payment/transfer/tx created.
- Network, ledger, and processor logs for the Connect success window show zero send, capture, withdrawal, broadcast, or spend-approval.
- Automated tests cover: success, retry/replay, webhook duplicate, client resume, and offline-then-sync; none produce a send or spend.
- A signature/challenge used in Connect cannot be executed as a transfer or allowance; attempting to treat it as one is rejected.
- QA and security review sign off that Connect success handlers contain no payment, wallet-send, or spend-approval calls.
- Support can confirm from audit logs that a given Connect success moved no value.
- Feature flags or experiments on Connect cannot enable an automatic send/spend on success.

## Out of scope

- The separate, explicit Send / Spend / Pay flows after a user is already connected
- Funding, deposits, or receive addresses shown after Connect
- Disconnect, revoke, and re-auth UX except where they must also avoid send/spend
- Pricing, rewards, or marketing copy unrelated to the no-send invariant
- New payment methods, chains, or processors beyond ensuring Connect success still never sends or spends on them
- Recovery of historical incidents where Connect already moved funds (ops runbook, not this change)

## Requirements

Contract for ticket #2635. Numbered **R-1–R-12**. Product-locked to Connect surfaces in `seanhogg/builderforce.ai`. This BA slot is **spec-only**: no application patch, no VSIX, no `specs/tasks/task-2635.md` dump.

### Definitions

- **Connect** is any linking / session-establishment path whose user-visible outcome is “connected”: mailbox OAuth (`GET /api/mailbox/connect/:provider` → provider consent → `GET /api/mailbox/callback/:provider` → `saveMailboxConnection` → `?mailbox=connected`), connector credential save (`createConnection`), social-account linking (via `connector_connections` / `SocialAccountView`), ad-account linking (via `connector_connections` / `AdAccountView`), marketing sender create/verify (`createSender` / `verifySender`), calendar OAuth, and any similarly named payment-processor Connect onboarding. Success may persist identity, account reference, sealed tokens, read capabilities, and a non-spend session.
- **Send / spend** is any movement of value or outbound delivery to a third party: mailbox send (`sendFromMailbox` / provider send), campaign delivery (`startCampaign` / `runCampaignBatch` / SMS send), social publish (`publishSocialPost` / `startSocialCampaign`), ad launch or budgeted write (`launchAdsCampaign` or equivalent spend write), payment-processor charge / PaymentIntent / transfer / payout, connector `runAction` that posts/charges/transfers, gas sponsorship that debits the user, or partner callbacks that execute a payment.
- **Allowed non-value side effects of Connect:** session/grant persistence, inbox push watch (`ensureMailboxWatch` — subscribe-to-notify, not send), a live **read-only** connection test/ping (`testConnection` / `get_me` / list-campaigns read), and non-value metadata (status=`connected`, `lastSyncedAt`, `missingFields`, `ready`).

### R-1 — Connect is link-only (execution invariant)

On Connect success the system SHALL persist identity, account reference, sealed credentials, read capabilities, and a non-spend session only. It MUST NOT create a transfer, payment, withdrawal, on-chain send, card charge, campaign send, social publish, ad launch, mailbox send, or equivalent spend. Completing Connect against a funded mailbox, ad account, or payment method MUST leave balances, allowances, inboxes-as-sent, and on-chain/off-chain ledgers unchanged except non-value metadata.

### R-2 — Mailbox Connect MUST NOT enable or exercise send

Mailbox OAuth callback SHALL call `saveMailboxConnection` (and MAY best-effort `ensureMailboxWatch`) and then redirect `?mailbox=connected` (or an error/decline query). It MUST NOT call send APIs, MUST NOT invoke `startCampaign` / `runCampaignBatch`, and MUST NOT set `allowSending=true`.

- First-time insert MUST persist `allowSending=false` (campaigns MUST NOT treat a newly connected mailbox as a sending identity).
- Reconnect (`onConflictDoUpdate`) MUST NOT flip `allowSending` as a side effect of OAuth success; reconnect recovers `status=connected` only.
- Campaigns and `sendFromMailbox` MUST refuse a mailbox with `allowSending=false`.
- Turning a mailbox into a sending identity is a **later, dedicated** manager-gated `PATCH /api/mailbox/connections/:id` with `{ allowSending: true }` (`setMailboxSending`). That PATCH is not Connect and MUST NOT run as part of Connect success.

Provider OAuth scopes on the mailbox grant MAY include later-send capability (Graph/Gmail send scopes) because the same sealed grant is what an **explicit** later send uses. Connect success MUST NOT exercise those scopes. Narrowing mailbox OAuth to read-only (forcing a second consent for send) is **out of scope** for this ticket unless Design elects it; the execution invariant still holds.

### R-3 — No bundled spend authorization on Connect

The Connect request, approval UI, granted **product** capabilities, and success payload MUST NOT include spend, transfer, unlimited-sign, or “send now” permissions that the product will exercise because Connect succeeded.

- Social Connect MAY store a publish-capable grant; `publishMode` / `ready` / `missingFields` describe whether a **later** publish could work. Connect MUST NOT publish.
- Ads Connect MAY store a spend-capable grant; `ready` / `missingFields` MUST say so **before** anyone is asked to spend. Connect MUST NOT launch or mutate billed campaigns.
- Connector `createConnection` stores credentials. It MUST NOT `runAction` except an optional read-only `testConnection` ping. A ping that would send, charge, or post MUST be skipped or must fail the test without executing the spend.
- Spend authority for value movement is obtained only in a later dedicated flow (campaign **Start/Send**, social **Publish**, ads **Launch**, mailbox send, payment confirm).

### R-4 — No automatic follow-on execution

Connect success MUST NOT enqueue or trigger send/spend workers, “complete setup by paying,” airdrop claims that move user funds, user-debit gas sponsorship, or partner/SDK/deeplink callbacks that execute a payment or send. `c.executionCtx.waitUntil(ensureMailboxWatch(…))` is the documented exception (inbox push only). Webhooks and SDK events fired because Connect succeeded MUST NOT invoke send/spend endpoints and MUST contain no payment/transfer/send identifiers.

A user MUST be able to Connect successfully and leave the product with **zero** delayed send/spend attributable to that success.

### R-5 — Separate confirmation for value movement

Any send or spend REQUIRES its own explicit user (or manager) confirmation **after** Connect has already reached success, or independently of Connect. Reusing the Connect signature, biometric, OAuth `state` nonce, callback `code`, or Connect success event id as a spend authorization is forbidden.

Concrete later flows (not triggered by Connect):

| Later action | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Email/SMS campaign send | `startCampaign` / `runCampaignBatch` (manager-gated marketing) |
| Mailbox one-off send | send path on `/api/mailbox/connections/:id` after `allowSending=true` |
| Social publish | publish / `startSocialCampaign` |
| Ad spend | launch / budgeted campaign write |
| Connector effect | `runAction` |
| Payment | dedicated PaymentIntent / charge / transfer confirm |

### R-6 — Idempotent and side-effect-free retries

Repeating Connect, reconnecting, handling duplicate success events, double-submit of the callback, or replaying the OAuth `code`/`state` MUST NOT produce a send or spend. Duplicate mailbox callback SHALL upsert the grant in place (natural key tenant+user+provider+account) without sending. Cancel, timeout, provider `error` decline, network drop, and `?mailbox=error|declined|no_account` paths MUST produce zero send/spend.

### R-7 — Honest UI and API

Success screens, emails, webhooks, redirects, and API responses for Connect MUST describe linking/session only (`?mailbox=connected`, connection `status: connected`, social/ads `SocialAccountView` / `AdAccountView`). They MUST NOT show amounts sent, “payment complete,” spend receipts, campaign send counts, or published-post permalinks as a consequence of Connect. `ready: false` / `missingFields` on a just-connected social or ads account is honest non-spend state, not a send.

### R-8 — Fail closed

If Connect cannot complete without attaching a spend payload, pre-signed transfer, auto-start campaign, auto-publish, or auto-launch, Connect MUST fail **before** success. It MUST NOT succeed “and also send.” A connector test that cannot ping without executing a spend MUST fail or skip the test; it MUST NOT run the spend to “prove” the connection.

### R-9 — Auditability

Every Connect success SHALL be logged as a **non-financial linking** event (mailbox connected, connector connection created, social/ads account linked, sender created/verified). Send/spend events SHALL be a distinct audit type (campaign recipient ledger, mailbox send, social publish result, ads insights/spend write, payment). A funds-out / send record MUST NEVER use a Connect success event id as its cause/parent.

### R-10 — Surface matrix (in scope)

QA and implementation MUST cover, for first connect, reconnect, expired/revoked session, multiple accounts, zero-balance and funded accounts:

1. Mailbox OAuth (Gmail / Microsoft 365) — connect, decline, error, reconnect.
2. Connector `createConnection` (including social and ads connector keys).
3. Social account linking vs publish.
4. Ad account linking vs launch/spend.
5. Marketing `createSender` / `verifySender` vs `startCampaign`.
6. Calendar OAuth, if present on the same Connect UX.
7. Payment-processor Connect onboarding, if present on the billing surface.

Each cell: **zero** value movement attributable to Connect success.

### R-11 — Agent, MCP, and canvas tools

Agent/MCP/canvas tools that wrap Connect (`mailbox.list_connections` connect URL, connector save, social/ads connect) MUST obey R-1–R-8. A tool MUST NOT chain Connect success into send/publish/launch/startCampaign in the same turn unless the user issued a **separate** explicit send/spend instruction after Connect already succeeded. “Connect and send the campaign” is two actions; Connect success alone is not authorization for the second.

### R-12 — Out of this contract (non-requirements)

- Design of later explicit send/spend/confirm-payment flows, except that they MUST NOT be triggered by Connect success (R-4, R-5).
- Custody model, yield, investing, and marketing copy that is not part of Connect execution.
- Third-party scams outside in-product Connect (in-product Connect still fails closed per R-8).
- Historical backfill of past incidents.
- Forcing a second OAuth consent to strip mailbox send scopes (R-2 last paragraph).
- Product-repo patch, VSIX, or `specs/tasks/` dump on this BA slot.

**Success for this ticket:** Connect success never sends or spends. The acceptance criteria in the PRD header remain the pass/fail tests; R-1–R-12 are the binding SHALL list for Design and implementation.
