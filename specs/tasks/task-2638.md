# PRD: C3 — Human-confirm send gate

## Problem & Goal

Campaign send, schedule, publish, and ads launch can fire from chat assent, agent tools, or a successful channel connect. That is unsafe: operators can spend or message a live audience without seeing channel, consent, sender, timing, copy, or budget.

**Goal:** Every live send/schedule/publish/ads launch requires an explicit human confirm control. Reuse Growth `draft → schedule → send` and Canvas send-readiness. Gate on C1 `guidedCampaignRun().maySend`. Chat assent, connect, and cloud agents never send or spend.

## Target users / ICP roles

- Growth / lifecycle operators running email, SMS, push, and in-product campaigns
- Performance marketers launching or publishing ads
- Founders / marketers using guided campaign runs in chat or Canvas
- Anonymous visitors (must not send; sign-in required)
- Cloud agents (draft-only; tools remain user-gated)

## Scope

In scope: wire a human-confirm send gate on existing `campaign.send`, `campaign.schedule`, publish, and ads launch paths. Confirm UI must show channel, audience consent summary, sender, schedule vs now, copy preview, and ads budget (when applicable). Enforce `guidedCampaignRun().maySend`. Anonymous and cloud-agent paths stay non-sending.

Out of scope items are listed at the end.

## Functional requirements

### 1. Confirm is the only send path

- `campaign.send`, `campaign.schedule`, publish, and ads launch MUST NOT execute until the operator completes the confirm control for that action.
- Chat messages that sound like assent (“yes”, “send it”, “go live”) MUST NOT send, schedule, publish, or spend.
- Successful channel / ad-account / identity connect MUST NOT send or spend. Connect may unlock the confirm control only when `maySend` is true and the operator is signed in.
- Cloud agents remain draft-only. Agent tools that would send, schedule, publish, or launch ads stay user-gated and MUST NOT auto-invoke confirm or execute those actions.

### 2. Gate on C1 `guidedCampaignRun().maySend`

- If `guidedCampaignRun().maySend` is false, confirm is hidden or disabled; send/schedule/publish/ads launch are blocked. Draft, edit, and preview remain available.
- If `maySend` is true and the user is authenticated, confirm is enabled for the current campaign/run.
- Re-check `maySend` at confirm submit. A stale true MUST NOT send.

### 3. Anonymous users

- Confirm and all send/schedule/publish/ads launch controls are disabled.
- Show a sign-in prompt, not a silent no-op.
- After sign-in, do not auto-send. Re-evaluate `maySend` and require a fresh confirm.

### 4. Confirm control contents

The confirm surface MUST present, before the operator can commit:

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Channel | Destination (email, SMS, push, in-product, ad network, etc.) |
| Audience consent summary | Who will be reached and on what consent/legal basis (counts, lists/segments, opt-in state; suppress unconsented) |
| Sender | From name, address, number, page, or ad account that will appear |
| Schedule vs now | Immediate send vs scheduled time (timezone visible) |
| Copy preview | Final payload the audience or ad will see (subject + body / primary text / creative summary) |
| Ads budget | Campaign/ad-set budget, currency, and billing period when the action spends |

Missing required fields block confirm. Operator can cancel with no side effects.

### 5. Reuse existing readiness, do not fork send logic

- Growth: keep `draft → schedule → send`. Confirm sits on schedule and send; it does not replace draft or invent a parallel pipeline.
- Canvas: reuse send-readiness. Confirm is available only when Canvas readiness is green **and** `maySend` is true.
- Commit through existing send / schedule / publish / ads launch APIs after confirm. Do not add a second execution path.

### 6. Confirm commit behavior

- Primary actions: **Send now**, **Schedule**, **Publish**, **Launch ads** — only those that apply.
- Commit is an explicit control click (or equivalent accessible activation), not a chat reply, not connect success, not agent tool success.
- On success: run the existing action; show confirmation of what ran (channel, audience, time, budget if ads).
- On failure: no partial send/spend; surface the error; campaign stays draft/scheduled as before the attempt.
- Idempotent: double-submit does not double-send or double-spend.

### 7. Agent and chat contract

- Models/agents may draft, preview, and *propose* send/schedule/publish/launch.
- They MUST NOT call send/schedule/publish/ads launch tools without the user-gated confirm succeeding in the same human session.
- Cloud agents cannot satisfy confirm on behalf of the user.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With `maySend === true` and a signed-in user, Growth send/schedule and Canvas publish/ads launch are blocked until confirm is completed with all required fields visible.
2. Confirm shows channel, audience consent summary, sender, schedule vs now, copy preview, and ads budget when the action can spend.
3. Chat assent does not send, schedule, publish, or spend, including after “looks good” / “send it” / similar.
4. Anonymous users see disabled send controls and a sign-in prompt; no send or spend.
5. After sign-in or successful connect, nothing is sent or spent until a new explicit confirm.
6. Cloud agents can draft only; send/schedule/publish/ads tools remain user-gated and do not execute from the agent.
7. `maySend === false` disables confirm; draft/preview still work.
8. Canvas send-readiness false keeps confirm unavailable even if `maySend` is true.
9. Confirm commit uses existing Growth `draft → schedule → send` and Canvas launch/publish paths; no duplicate send pipeline.
10. Failed or cancelled confirm leaves no messages sent and no ad spend.
11. Double-click / retry of a successful confirm does not duplicate send or spend.
12. Schedule vs now is explicit; a scheduled campaign does not send immediately, and Send now does not silently schedule.

## Out of scope

- Changing C1 `guidedCampaignRun().maySend` semantics or how it is computed
- New campaign types, channels, or ad networks
- Redesigning Growth draft/schedule/send or Canvas readiness internals beyond inserting the gate
- Consent capture, preference-center, or list-hygiene product work (confirm only *displays* the summary)
- Budget optimization, bidding, or creative generation
- Multi-approver / SOC2-style approval workflows
- Letting cloud agents confirm or send
- Auto-send on connect, OAuth, or provider webhook
- Analytics, attribution, or post-send reporting changes
- Localization of the confirm copy beyond existing product i18n patterns
- Mobile-native confirm UI (responsive web of the existing surfaces is enough)

### R-2 Confirm enablement (pre-confirm) vs `maySend` (post-confirm)

**Confirm is enabled** (`confirmEnabled`) only when **all** of the following are true for the current campaign/run:

| Conjunct | Source | Required for confirm to show/enable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed-in **human operator** in this session | auth | yes |
| Not anonymous | auth | yes |
| Not a cloud agent | actor | yes |
| `persistence === "server"` | C1 | yes |
| `agentDraftOnly === false` | C1 | yes |
| `checklist.ready === true` | C1 journey checklist | yes |
| `sendReadiness.ready === true` | Growth / Canvas send-readiness (C1) | yes |
| Required confirm fields present for **this** action (R-5) | C3 | yes |
| Payload is current (no unsaved edit that would desync the preview) | C3 | yes |
| `humanConfirmed === true` | C1 | **no** — that is the output of confirm |

**`maySend` (C1, unchanged)** is true only when the enablement conjuncts **plus** `humanConfirmed === true` all hold.

- If `confirmEnabled` is false: confirm is **hidden or disabled**; send / schedule / publish / ads launch are **blocked**; draft, edit, and preview remain available (except anonymous, R-9).
- If `confirmEnabled` is true: confirm is enabled for **this** campaign/run and **this** action. Send still does not run until confirm commit succeeds and `maySend` is re-checked true (R-12).
- Stale UI state is not a permit. Recompute `confirmEnabled` at open and at submit.
- Canvas: `sendReadiness.ready === false` keeps confirm unavailable **even if** a stale `maySend === true` is observed (R-15).

### R-3 Confirm is the only send path (server-enforced)

These existing actions **MUST NOT execute** until a valid, unconsumed, payload-bound confirm for **that** action exists (R-6, R-12):

- Growth `campaign.send` (send now)
- Growth `campaign.schedule` (or the existing schedule seam — `scheduledAt` on the current draft→schedule→send pipeline; Architect binds the exact symbol)
- Canvas / social **publish** (`social_campaign.publish` or the existing Canvas publish seam)
- **Ads launch** (the existing Canvas/Growth ads-launch seam — Architect binds the exact symbol; C3 does **not** invent a new ads buyer)

Non-negotiable:

1. **UI disable is not sufficient.** The server / tool handlers for the four actions above MUST reject (no send, no spend, structured error) without a valid confirm.
2. Chat messages that sound like assent (“yes”, “send it”, “go live”, “looks good”, “ship it”, “do it”) MUST NOT send, schedule, publish, or spend (R-8).
3. Successful channel / ad-account / identity / mailbox / OAuth **connect** MUST NOT send or spend (R-10). Connect may only unlock confirm when `confirmEnabled` is true.
4. Cloud agents remain draft-only (R-11). They cannot satisfy confirm and MUST NOT auto-invoke the four actions or set `humanConfirmed`.

Draft, edit, preview, list, and connect stay available without confirm.

### R-4 Reuse existing pipelines — do not fork send logic

1. **Growth:** keep `draft → schedule → send`. Confirm sits **on schedule and on send only**. It does not replace draft and does not invent a parallel pipeline.
2. **Canvas:** reuse send-readiness. Confirm is available only when Canvas `sendReadiness.ready === true` **and** the other `confirmEnabled` conjuncts hold.
3. After confirm commit, call the **existing** send / schedule / publish / ads-launch APIs. Do **not** add a second execution path, a second queue, or a new campaign type.
4. Gate **only** send paths that already exist. Do not add push, in-product, or new ad networks in C3. If a channel has no send path today, it is out of scope (not a missing confirm field).
5. Architect names the exact existing functions. If ads launch does not exist in the bound tree, Design must say so — C3 then gates send/schedule/publish only and does **not** stub an ads buyer.

### R-5 Confirm control contents (all required before commit)

The confirm surface MUST present, before the operator can commit, the following for **this** action. Missing required fields **block** confirm (control disabled + reason). Cancel has no side effects.

| Field | Requirement | Required when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Channel** | Destination of this action (email, SMS, social network, ad network, etc. — whatever this action actually uses) | always |
| **Audience consent summary** | Who will be reached and on what consent/legal basis: reachable **count**, list/segment identity, opt-in state. Unconsented / suppressed recipients are **excluded** from the reachable count (display-only; C3 does not capture consent or mutate lists). Empty reachable audience **blocks** commit. | always |
| **Sender** | From name, address, number, page, or ad account that will appear to the audience | always |
| **Schedule vs now** | Immediate send vs scheduled instant, with **timezone** visible | always |
| **Copy preview** | Final payload the audience or ad will see (subject + body / primary text / creative summary, as applicable to the channel) | always |
| **Ads budget** | Campaign/ad-set budget, **currency**, and billing period | **only** when the action spends (ads launch). Non-spend send/schedule/publish MUST NOT block on a missing budget. |

Operator can **Cancel** with no side effects (`humanConfirmed` stays false; no send/spend).

### R-6 Payload-bound `humanConfirmed` (C3 is the only writer of true)

1. C3 is the **only** writer of `humanConfirmed = true`. C1 reads it. Cloud agents, chat assent, connect callbacks, and webhooks MUST NOT write it.
2. `humanConfirmed` is bound to a tuple: `(campaignOrRunId, actionKind, payloadFingerprint)` where `actionKind` ∈ `{ send_now, schedule, publish, launch_ads }` and `payloadFingerprint` covers channel, audience/consent snapshot, sender, schedule vs now, copy, and ads budget (when applicable).
3. Any change to those fields after confirm **invalidates** `humanConfirmed` (treat as false). A fresh confirm is required. Confirm-then-edit-then-send with the old stamp is a defect.
4. Confirm for **Send now** does not authorize **Schedule**, and vice versa. Same for publish vs ads launch.
5. `humanConfirmed = false` is the default. Cancel, failed field checks, and fingerprint mismatch leave it false.

### R-7 Commit actions and activation semantics

Primary actions, **only those that apply** to this campaign/run:

- **Send now** → existing Growth send
- **Schedule** → existing Growth schedule
- **Publish** → existing Canvas/social publish
- **Launch ads** → existing ads launch (when that seam exists)

Commit is an **explicit control click** (or equivalent accessible activation: keyboard, labelled button, `aria` on the confirm control). It is **not** a chat reply, **not** connect success, **not** agent tool success, **not** a page refresh, **not** a webhook.

### R-8 Chat assent never sends or spends

1. Natural-language assent — including “yes”, “send it”, “go live”, “looks good”, “ship it”, “do it”, “confirm”, “ok” — MUST NOT send, schedule, publish, or spend.
2. Models/agents may draft, preview, and **propose** send/schedule/publish/launch. Proposal copy is not confirm.
3. The C0 prompt already describes intake→checklist→copy→confirm. C3 wires the control; it does not treat a model saying “confirmed” as the operator’s confirm.

### R-9 Anonymous users

1. Confirm and all send / schedule / publish / ads-launch controls are **disabled**.
2. Show a **sign-in prompt** (existing auth), not a silent no-op and not a fake confirm.
3. After sign-in: **do not auto-send**. Re-evaluate `confirmEnabled`. Require a **fresh** confirm in the authenticated session.
4. Anonymous draft/preview may continue on C1 `persistence !== "server"` / draft-only paths. Those paths never send.

### R-10 Connect never sends or spends

Successful channel, mailbox, ad-account, sender-identity, or OAuth connect:

- MUST NOT send, schedule, publish, or spend.
- MUST NOT set `humanConfirmed`.
- MAY unlock the confirm control only when `confirmEnabled` becomes true **and** the operator is a signed-in human.
- Provider webhooks / OAuth return URLs follow the same rule.

### R-11 Cloud agents remain draft-only

1. Cloud agents may draft, edit, preview, and propose. They MUST NOT execute send / schedule / publish / ads launch.
2. Agent tools for those four actions stay **user-gated**: they execute only after this ticket’s human confirm succeeds in the **same human session**. The agent MUST NOT call them because the user typed assent.
3. Cloud agents **cannot** satisfy confirm on behalf of the user and MUST NOT write `humanConfirmed = true`.
4. `agentDraftOnly === true` ⇒ `confirmEnabled === false` ⇒ `maySend === false`.

### R-12 Confirm commit sequence (order is load-bearing)

On confirm submit, in this order:

1. Recompute `confirmEnabled` on **current** state. If false → refuse; no side effects.
2. Verify all R-5 fields for this `actionKind`. If any required field is missing → refuse; no side effects.
3. Persist `humanConfirmed = true` bound to the R-6 tuple (run, action, fingerprint).
4. Recompute C1 `maySend` on **current** state. A stale pre-submit true MUST NOT send.
5. If `maySend === true`: invoke the **existing** API for this `actionKind` **once**, with an idempotency key / confirm id that the send path consumes at most once.
6. If `maySend === false`: do **not** send or spend; surface why; campaign remains draft/scheduled as before the attempt. Do not leave a half-written send.

On success: run that existing action; show confirmation of what ran (channel, audience reachable count, time, budget if ads).

### R-13 Failure, cancel, idempotency

1. **Cancel:** no messages, no spend, `humanConfirmed` stays false.
2. **Failure** (validation, `maySend` false, upstream API error): no **partial** send/spend; surface the error; campaign stays in the state it had before the attempt (draft or already-scheduled).
3. **Idempotent:** double-click, retry, or replay of a **successful** confirm MUST NOT double-send or double-spend. Second activation sees in-flight or already-consumed confirm id and no-ops the execution (may show the original success).
4. Retry after **failure** requires a new confirm only if the fingerprint changed or `humanConfirmed` was cleared; otherwise a single unconsumed confirm may be retried **once the error is gone**, still without duplicating a successful send.

### R-14 Schedule vs now is explicit

1. Confirm must make **Send now** vs **Schedule** distinct (R-5, R-7).
2. A scheduled campaign MUST NOT send immediately.
3. Send now MUST NOT silently schedule.
4. Timezone is visible on the confirm surface.
5. Growth `draft → schedule → send` remains the pipeline; confirm does not collapse schedule into send.

### R-15 Canvas send-readiness is an independent gate

1. Canvas confirm is unavailable when `sendReadiness.ready === false`, even if a stale `maySend === true` is observed.
2. Canvas confirm is also unavailable when any other `confirmEnabled` conjunct is false.
3. Growth send-readiness (suppression, unsubscribe, transport) is the Growth half of the same C1 `sendReadiness` flag — do not fork a second readiness object in C3.

### R-16 Out of scope (do not pull in)

- Changing C1 `maySend` semantics or how it is computed
- New campaign types, channels, or ad networks
- Redesigning Growth draft/schedule/send or Canvas readiness internals beyond inserting this gate
- Consent capture, preference-center, or list-hygiene (confirm **displays** the summary only)
- Budget optimization, bidding, or creative generation
- Multi-approver / SOC2-style approval workflows
- Letting cloud agents confirm or send
- Auto-send on connect, OAuth, or provider webhook
- Analytics, attribution, or post-send reporting
- Localization beyond existing i18n patterns
- Mobile-native confirm UI (responsive web of existing surfaces is enough)
- C4 / ICP picker (#2636 / #2640) — not a confirm gate
- Dumping `specs/tasks/task-2638.md` or product-repo files from this BA slot
