# Product Requirements Document (PRD) for Backlog Refinement Cadence Documentation

## Problem & Goal

### Problem
- **Lack of Structured Process**: There is currently no documented backlog refinement process or established bi-weekly meeting cadence for the Agile Ceremonies (AC-7).
- **Inconsistent Practices**: Without a standardized approach, teams may experience inconsistent refinement practices, leading to potential misalignment and inefficiencies.
- **Missed Opportunities**: Lack of a regular cadence may result in delayed identification of dependencies, risks, and impediments, impacting sprint planning and execution.

### Goal
- **Establish a Standard Process**: Document a clear and consistent backlog refinement process.
- **Implement a Bi-Weekly Cadence**: Define and implement a bi-weekly meeting schedule for backlog refinement sessions.
- **Improve Team Alignment**: Ensure all team members understand the process and their roles in backlog refinement.

## Target Users / ICP Roles

- **Product Owners**: Responsible for prioritizing the backlog and ensuring it is ready for upcoming sprints.
- **Scrum Masters**: Facilitators who ensure the refinement process is followed and meetings are productive.
- **Development Team Members**: Contributors who provide insights into technical feasibility and effort estimation.
- **Stakeholders**: Individuals who need to be informed about the progress and priorities of the project.

## Scope

- **Process Documentation**: Create a comprehensive guide outlining the backlog refinement process, including objectives, roles, and responsibilities.
- **Meeting Cadence**: Establish a bi-weekly meeting schedule for backlog refinement sessions.
- **Tools and Templates**: Identify and document any tools or templates that will be used to facilitate the refinement process.
- **Training and Onboarding**: Develop training materials and onboarding processes for new team members to understand the refinement process.

## Functional Requirements

1. **Process Documentation**
   - Define the purpose and objectives of backlog refinement.
   - Outline the steps involved in the refinement process.
   - Specify roles and responsibilities for each team member.
   - Include guidelines for prioritizing and estimating backlog items.

2. **Meeting Cadence**
   - Schedule bi-weekly backlog refinement meetings.
   - Define the duration and agenda for each meeting.
   - Establish guidelines for meeting preparation and follow-up.

3. **Tools and Templates**
   - Identify tools for scheduling, collaboration, and documentation (e.g., Jira, Confluence).
   - Create templates for meeting agendas, notes, and action items.

4. **Training and Onboarding**
   - Develop training materials covering the refinement process and meeting guidelines.
   - Implement an onboarding checklist for new team members to review the process.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Documentation**: The backlog refinement process is documented in a central location accessible to all team members.
- **Cadence**: Bi-weekly backlog refinement meetings are scheduled and communicated to all stakeholders.
- **Tools and Templates**: All necessary tools and templates are identified, implemented, and tested.
- **Training**: All current team members have completed the training, and onboarding materials are available for new members.
- **Feedback Loop**: A mechanism is in place for team members to provide feedback on the refinement process and suggest improvements.

## Out of Scope

- **Sprint Planning and Review Meetings**: This document does not cover the processes for sprint planning or sprint review meetings.
- **Daily Stand-ups**: The daily stand-up meeting process is not included in this document.
- **Retrospectives**: The retrospective process is not covered.
- **Tool Customization**: Customization of tools beyond basic setup and configuration is not included.
- **Advanced Estimation Techniques**: This document does not cover advanced techniques for backlog estimation and prioritization.
