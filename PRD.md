# Product Requirements Document (PRD) for GAP-G1 Task Closure

## Problem & Goal

### Problem
The GAP-G1 task was marked as Closed in the workstream tracker (task #144) without a clear pass/fail conclusion. This lack of clarity hinders the ability to assess the task's outcome, understand its impact, and plan subsequent actions.

### Goal
To provide a clear pass/fail conclusion for the GAP-G1 task, ensuring that the outcome is well-documented and actionable for future reference and decision-making.

## Target Users / ICP Roles

- **Project Managers**: To assess the task's impact on the project timeline and deliverables.
- **Quality Assurance (QA) Engineers**: To understand the task's outcome and its implications for product quality.
- **Developers**: To know if the task requires further work or if it can be considered complete.
- **Stakeholders**: To have visibility into the task's status and its contribution to the overall project goals.

## Scope

- **Documentation**: Update the workstream tracker with a clear pass/fail conclusion.
- **Communication**: Notify relevant team members and stakeholders of the task's outcome.
- **Analysis**: Provide a brief analysis of the task's impact on the project.
- **Next Steps**: Outline any follow-up actions required as a result of the task's closure.

## Functional Requirements

1. **Update Workstream Tracker**
   - Record the pass/fail conclusion in the GAP-G1 task entry.
   - Include a brief description of the criteria used to determine the outcome.

2. **Notification**
   - Send a notification to the project team and relevant stakeholders.
   - Include the pass/fail conclusion and a link to the updated task entry.

3. **Analysis**
   - Provide a short analysis of how the task's outcome affects the project timeline and deliverables.
   - Highlight any dependencies or related tasks that may be impacted.

4. **Next Steps**
   - List any follow-up actions required as a result of the task's closure.
   - Assign responsibilities and set deadlines for these actions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The GAP-G1 task entry in the workstream tracker is updated with a clear pass/fail conclusion.
- The project team and relevant stakeholders have been notified of the task's outcome.
- A brief analysis of the task's impact is documented and accessible.
- A list of next steps is provided, with responsibilities and deadlines clearly assigned.

## Out of Scope

- **Retrospective Analysis**: Conducting a detailed retrospective of the task's execution is not required.
- **Process Changes**: Implementing changes to the task closure process based on this task is out of scope.
- **Additional Testing**: Re-testing or re-evaluating the task's outcome is not part of this effort.
- **External Communication**: Communicating the task's outcome to external parties or clients is not included.
