About the app

What Builderforce.ai does for a founder

Builderforce.ai is a web application for turning an idea into a company investors can underwrite. You describe what you want on a visual canvas, and your team and AI agents build it, run it, measure it, and assemble the evidence — the model, the metrics, the deck, the data room — in one workspace instead of a dozen disconnected tools.

Connected-account access is granted per workspace, used only for the features you switch on, and can be revoked at any time. Privacy policy · Terms of service

Open it without an account
Every page on this site, and a working canvas you can type into, is available to anyone. You sign in only when you want to save your work, invite teammates, or connect a tool.
A solo founder does not have a CFO. They have the CFO seat.
Agents are hired into a workspace like teammates and hold a role: CFO, CMO, CTO, CEO. They pick up the work, do it, and hand it back for review — so a person decides what actually ships, and the seat is already there when you hire the human who fills it.
Connect the numbers you already have
With your permission, Builderforce.ai connects to Stripe, Xero, QuickBooks, Google Drive, Gmail and Calendar — so the runway chart, the cohort table and the model in your deck are read from the ledger rather than retyped into a spreadsheet.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to start?
No. The first canvas is private and local to your browser — nothing leaves the device until you sign in. When you do, the draft you already started is claimed into your workspace rather than discarded.
What exactly are the AI agents?
Specialists that hold a role on your team — a CFO, a CMO, a CTO, and others. They sit along the bottom of every canvas beside the people you invited. Bring one onto the board and it reads the work, takes the assignment, and produces a change you can review.
Where do the numbers in my deck come from?
Your ledger. Connect Stripe, Xero or QuickBooks and the runway, burn, ARR and cohort figures are computed from the transactions and carry the date they were read. A figure with no source behind it is flagged before an investor finds it.
Who can see my data room?
Only the people you invite, on the terms you set: an NDA to sign, a watermark carrying their name, an expiry date, and a record of every file they opened. Access is granted per investor and can be revoked at any time.
What happens to work an agent does on its own?
Every change has an author and a diff. Anything that touches production, sends on your behalf, or exceeds a spending limit waits for a named human approver on the board.
