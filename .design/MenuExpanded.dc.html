Why it is a column, not a tab bar
Sixteen sub-views. A horizontal tab bar holds five before it truncates or scrolls, and the ones it hides are the ones a founder came for — Meetings, Co-founder, Performance, Plan. The index runs down the panel's left edge instead, so every sub-view is visible at once and the count is allowed to grow.
This is also where the Team plan starts. Roles, Teams and Approvals are the rows a founder reaches for the first time they need a second pair of hands — and inviting that person is the moment the workspace stops being one seat.

Thirteen of these rows — Investors and Finance among them — had no icon of their own and all drew the generic four-square glyph, because NavIcon resolves a row by its group id and an unmapped id falls through to apps. Each now has one. A row shows a count only when the number is actionable: unread in Inbox, live projects in Projects, investors waiting on you in Investors.
