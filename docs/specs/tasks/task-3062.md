# PRD: SOC 2 Type I Audit

**Status:** Planned  
**Priority:** P1, Revenue  
**Target:** Q4 2026  
**Repo:** seanhogg/builderforce.ai (`main`)  
**Baseline:** investigation 2026-10-12 (chat 142). Re-verify every cited count before sign-off.

## Problem & Goal

Enterprise buyers treat an independent SOC 2 report as a procurement gate. Builderforce has no Type I engagement: no contracted CPA, no system description, no management assertion, no Type I report, and no trust-center certification artifact. `docs/compliance` is a privacy pack (DPA, incident response, subprocessors, DPIA), not an ISMS. `agent-runtime/docs/SECURITY.md` is vulnerability reporting only. There is no repo-root `CODEOWNERS`.

That gap is not filled by the product. Tenant SOC 2 tooling is largely built and must not be described as the audit. `SOC2_BASELINE` tracks 28 Common Criteria controls (CC1.1–CC1.4, CC2.1–CC2.3, CC3.1–CC3.4, CC4.1–CC4.2, CC5.1–CC5.3, CC6.1–CC6.3, CC6.6–CC6.7, CC7.1–CC7.4, CC8.1, CC9.1–CC9.2). Marketing FAQ says 30. `soc2Scan()` is labeled not a certification. The public `/soc2` page does not claim Builderforce is certified. PRD 08 Phase 7 (auto evidence, governance-auditor, merge blocker) is still Planned.

A Type I is an independent CPA examination of whether the description is fairly presented and whether controls are suitably designed and placed in operation at a point in time. Security is the required Trust Services Criterion. Design claims are currently indefensible:

- Logical isolation (CC6): 271 unscoped tenant queries, about 35 inserts without `segmentId`, no row-level security.
- Change and vulnerability management (CC7/CC8): esbuild GHSA affecting 6 packages, no CI migrate dry-run, 588 lint errors, no repo-root `CODEOWNERS`.
- Operations: production on Cloudflare Free and Neon Free with no SIEM export; Stripe secrets unset; worker `JWT_SECRET` residual open since the 2026-07-24 internal audit.

**Goal:** In Q4 2026, management can sign a Security-only Type I assertion that matches production, an independent CPA can examine that design as of a named date, and the issued report can be shared with customers under a restricted distribution policy. The product tracker may feed Builderforce’s own evidence pack. It is not the attestation.

## Target users / ICP roles

- **Management signer:** officer who owns the system description and the written assertion.
- **Compliance owner:** scope, CPA coordination, control matrix, policy approval record, evidence index, claim control.
- **Control owners:** engineering (isolation, change management, SDLC, dependency patching), security (monitoring, incident response, access reviews), and operations (secrets, hosting, backups, vendors).
- **External CPA firm:** examines design only; does not implement controls.
- **Customer security reviewers:** consumers of the issued report. They are why this is a revenue epic. They are not operators of the engagement and must not be given the readiness diagnostic as if it were a report.

## Scope

In scope is the company examination of Builderforce production as of one Q4 2026 date, Security TSC only.

- Contract an independent licensed CPA. Scope line: Type I, Security, named as-of date, named system boundary, subservice organizations (at minimum Cloudflare, Neon, Stripe, and GitHub), and carve-out versus inclusive method.
- System description that matches production, including free-tier hosting and the absence of a SIEM export.
- Approved policy set: ISMS, access control, change management, incident response, vendor management, SDLC, and backup. Privacy documents may be inputs. They are not the set.
- Control matrix for the criteria the CPA will examine, mapped to owners and design evidence. Reconcile the product’s 28-control baseline and the FAQ’s “30” to that set. Do not invent criteria to hit a number.
- Evidence that each in-scope control is designed and placed in operation on the as-of date. A manual export from `soc_controls` / `soc_evidence` for Builderforce’s own tenant may be one input.
- Remediate, or formally narrow and disclose, design defects that would make the description false. Re-count the 2026-10-12 findings before closure.
- Management assertion, fieldwork support, restricted retention of the report, and a freeze on public certification claims until issuance.
- Repo changes required for a true change-management and isolation design: repo-root `CODEOWNERS`, CI migrate dry-run, dependency patching, tenant-scope enforcement on paths the description claims are isolated, and closure of the secrets findings.

Boundaries the description must not overstate: tenant isolation, vulnerability handling, change control, monitoring, and secret management. Availability is not an examined category. The description still has to describe real hosting.

## Functional requirements

1. **Engagement.** Execute a CPA engagement letter for a SOC 2 Type I, Security only, with as-of date, boundary, locations, and subservice method written down. Record the firm, the signer, and the distribution terms. Do not schedule the as-of date before the design gate in requirement 6 is passed or explicitly risk-accepted by the signer.
2. **System description.** Produce the description the report will contain: services, software, infrastructure, data, people, procedures, trust-boundary, complementary user-entity controls, and subservice organizations. State Cloudflare Free, Neon Free, and no SIEM export if those facts are still true. List what is outside the boundary.
3. **Policies.** Publish the seven policies in Scope, each with owner, approver, and effective date on or before the as-of date. Incident response may extend the privacy IR document; it must cover the production system, not only privacy events. Vendor policy must cover subprocessors already listed for privacy plus any other operator inside the boundary.
4. **Control matrix.** One matrix, owned by compliance, with criterion, control statement, owner, design evidence, and open exception. Include the product baseline IDs that remain in scope, and any Security criteria the CPA adds. The matrix is the source for customer-facing control counts. Update the FAQ off “30” unless the examined set is actually 30.
5. **Evidence pack.** For each matrix row, store design evidence with a retrieval path the CPA can use: approved policy, config or workflow showing the control exists, owner, and as-of snapshot. Use the product export only for Builderforce’s own tenant. Keep customer tenant data out of the pack unless the CPA requires a specific artifact and management approves the disclosure. Label every product readiness output, including `soc2Scan()` and the security-agent sweep, as internal indicators.
6. **Design gate.** Before the as-of date, disposition each blocker. “Disposition” means remediated in the attested boundary, or removed from the description with a signed management exception. Open, undisclosed failures fail the gate.
   - Isolation claims require tenant scope on attested data paths (predicate and/or RLS) and `segmentId` on attested inserts. The 271 unscoped queries and about 35 missing-`segmentId` inserts are the baseline to re-count, not a permanent waiver. Implementation may sit on the Identity roadmap; this epic owns the go/no-go.
   - The esbuild GHSA in the six packages is patched, or a compensating control and signed risk acceptance are in the matrix.
   - CI runs a migrate dry-run on the attested change path. Change-control wording matches CI. If the control claims lint-clean merges, the 588 lint errors are burned down and the gate enforces that claim; otherwise the control statement is narrowed and the debt is tracked. It is not described as a clean gate.
   - Repo-root `CODEOWNERS` exists and is part of the change-management control.
   - Stripe secrets are set in the secret store, or Stripe processing is excluded from the description. The residual worker `JWT_SECRET` from 2026-07-24 is rotated or removed, and the worker auth secret in use is not that residual.
   - Monitoring wording matches what exists (including the weekly security sweep, if still used). Do not describe a SIEM export that does not exist.
7. **Assertion and report.** Management signs the Type I assertion only after the design gate. Support fieldwork with read-only or otherwise controlled access. Receive the CPA report. Store it in a restricted location. Do not commit the report, workpapers, or customer evidence to the public repo. Record opinion type, exceptions, and distribution rules.
8. **Claims.** Until the report is issued, `/soc2`, the FAQ, and sales materials stay non-certified. After issuance, customer sharing follows the report’s distribution terms and points at the report, not at `SOC2_AUDIT_ID`, the tracker, or the security agent. Product UI copy that says the diagnostic is not a certification stays.

## Acceptance criteria

- Engagement letter is executed for Type I / Security / named as-of date in Q4 2026 / named boundary and subservice method.
- System description is approved by the signer and matches verified production facts, including hosting tier and logging limits.
- Seven policies are approved with effective dates on or before the as-of date.
- Control matrix covers the CPA’s Security set; every row has an owner and a design-evidence link; the FAQ count equals that set, not an unreconciled 30.
- Design-gate recount is attached: unscoped tenant queries, inserts missing `segmentId`, RLS posture, esbuild GHSA status across the six packages, CI migrate dry-run, lint-versus-control-statement, repo-root `CODEOWNERS`, Stripe secrets, and the 2026-07-24 worker `JWT_SECRET` residual. Each item is closed or has a signed boundary exception. No attested isolation, change, vulnerability, secret, or monitoring claim lacks a placed-in-operation artifact.
- Management assertion is signed. A CPA-issued SOC 2 Type I report is in the restricted store, not in public git, with opinion, exceptions, and who may receive it recorded.
- Public pages do not call Builderforce SOC 2 certified before issuance. Product readiness surfaces still say they are not a certification.
- A Builderforce-tenant tracker export is producible as an input, and the evidence index states it is not the attestation.

An issued qualified or adverse opinion does not meet the revenue goal. If fieldwork would produce one, the as-of date moves until the design gate passes. Type I does not require a multi-month operating-effectiveness sample.

## Out of scope

- SOC 2 Type II, and any operating-effectiveness period.
- Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, and Privacy trust services criteria. Privacy documents stay inputs only.
- Buying a SIEM or leaving Cloudflare Free / Neon Free, unless the design gate cannot be passed without that change. A failed monitoring design is a blocker, not a silent upgrade program.
- Delivering the full Identity roadmap beyond the isolation defects inside the attested boundary.
- PRD 08 Phase 7: auto SOC 2 evidence, governance-auditor, and blocker-gates-merge.
- Rebuilding the tenant tracker, scanners, or `/security?sub=soc2` UI, except copy that would misstate certification or control count.
- Auditing customer tenants, issuing customer attestation letters, or letting prospects treat the readiness diagnostic as a report.
- ISO 27001 or other frameworks.
- Offensive testing, exploit development, or proof-of-concept code for the unscoped queries, the GHSA, or the residual secret. Remediation is patching, scoping, rotation, and accurate description.
