# Subprocessor register and provider review

The public list at `/legal/subprocessors` is the customer-facing register. The privacy owner maintains provider legal name, service, data categories, data-subject types, countries, hosting region, security review, DPA date, transfer mechanism, training terms, deletion/return behavior, incident SLA, and exit status in the governance vendor tracker.

No provider may receive customer prompts, chats, code, ideas, or personal data until the owner verifies: use is limited to service delivery; no sale; model training is disabled or requires a separate informed customer choice; retention/deletion terms match product promises; confidentiality and security duties flow down; incident cooperation exists; and SCCs/UK Addendum or another valid transfer mechanism is executed where required. Notify customers before material additions and operate an objection/escalation path.
