# WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility audit

Automated tests are a floor. Each release samples public, authentication, dashboard, chat/agent approval, settings, privacy forms, errors, and dialogs at desktop/mobile and 200–400% zoom.

Verify keyboard-only completion; visible, unobscured focus; skip link and landmarks; heading order; accessible names/descriptions/errors; semantic tables/forms; contrast and non-color cues; reflow and text spacing; target size; drag alternatives; authentication without cognitive-function tests; timeouts; reduced motion; captions/transcripts; screen-reader announcements; and modal focus restoration. Test NVDA+Chrome/Firefox, VoiceOver+Safari, and Windows high contrast at least quarterly.

Serious/critical issues block release unless the accessibility owner documents an accessible alternative, owner, due date, and risk acceptance. Feedback to accessibility@builderforce.ai is acknowledged, triaged, and linked to remediation evidence.
