# Privacy incident and breach response

Report suspected loss, unauthorized access/disclosure, tenant crossover, prompt leakage, compromised credentials, or processor incident immediately to the incident commander and privacy owner. Preserve evidence without expanding exposure.

1. Triage, contain, rotate/revoke, isolate affected workloads, and open a privileged incident record.
2. Determine data, people, jurisdictions, encryption status, acquisition/access, duration, processors, and ongoing risk. Maintain a decision log and chain of custody.
3. Engage counsel and insurers; obtain processor facts; assess notification thresholds and the shortest regulator/individual/customer deadlines. Notices must be accurate, accessible, and updated as facts develop.
4. Eradicate, recover, validate tenant isolation, monitor misuse, and supply practical protective steps.
5. Complete root cause, corrective actions, control tests, assessment updates, and board/leadership review. Retain the incident record even when notification is not required.

Exercise annually with a cross-tenant chat disclosure and a processor compromise scenario.
