# Minor safety, COPPA, sensitive data, and Colorado chatbot controls

BuilderForce is an adult business service and is not directed to children. Password registration requires an 18+ attestation. OAuth/magic-link onboarding must obtain the same attestation before agent/chat access. Known minors are blocked and their account/data referred to Privacy for deletion; do not attempt to obtain parental consent ad hoc.

Never solicit unnecessary sensitive data in prompts. Apply minimization, redaction, role-based access, restricted logs, short retention, encryption, and explicit purpose/consent where law requires. Prohibit sexual exploitation content, grooming, self-harm encouragement, illegal discrimination, manipulative dark patterns, and inference of sensitive traits for consequential decisions.

Before Colorado chatbot/minor provisions apply, validate conspicuous AI notice, crisis/escalation handling, minor detection that is proportionate and privacy-preserving, guardian/appeal paths where legally applicable, safety testing, incident reporting, and documentation of reasonable care. Re-review law effective dates quarterly; service availability to minors remains blocked unless Legal approves a dedicated compliant experience.
