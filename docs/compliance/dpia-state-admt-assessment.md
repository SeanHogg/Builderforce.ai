# DPIA, US state assessment, and California ADMT assessment

An assessment is mandatory before sensitive data processing, systematic monitoring, targeted advertising/sale, profiling with significant effects, large-scale processing, minors processing, biometric/geolocation use, or a materially new agentic capability.

Record purpose/necessity/proportionality; data flow and retention; people affected; benefits; reasonably foreseeable harms (privacy, bias, manipulation, security, economic/physical injury); data quality; model/provider; explainability; alternatives; human oversight; access/appeal; testing; mitigations; residual risk; owner/approver/review date. Consult affected stakeholders or the regulator where required.

California ADMT addendum: classify whether the system makes or substantially facilitates a significant decision; document logic, key parameters, intended output, training/evaluation data, validity/reliability, discrimination testing, cybersecurity, consumer notice, access/opt-out rights, human appeal, and why less intrusive alternatives are insufficient. Do not deploy a consequential use until Legal marks the assessment approved.
