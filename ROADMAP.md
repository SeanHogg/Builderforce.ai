## How an entry is written

One primary owner, one independently verifiable state, one acceptance criterion tied to the declared method — the arc **Idea → Make → Run → Measure** for WHERE it sits, the loop **Read → Prove → Build** for what it does. An item in Build names its proof form and the kill condition Measure will grade. Split compound bullets when their subclaims differ in owner or completion state.

Authenticated UI work is never a standalone page: it registers a destination or Canvas object and opens through the persistent shell, and must satisfy PRD 21 — canonical primitives/tokens, five locales, both themes, 360px, coarse-pointer and keyboard parity, visible focus, reduced motion, visible-disabled entitlements, bottom-centre Canvas composer.

**An entry names its own blocker or it is not blocked.** Scheduling around other work is not a blocker and does not belong here.

## North Star — **Idea to Real**

> Declared as spec data in two places and ratcheted by `frontend/scripts/check-methodology.mjs`. **This roadmap uses that vocabulary and no other.**
>
> **THE ARC — `Idea → Make → Run → Measure` (→ Reach → Expand).** Where you are. Owned by `STAGES` in [navGroups.ts](./frontend/src/lib/navGroups.ts). (Renamed from `Market` — it collided with the Marketplace commerce surface; `Growth` was considered next but collided with the existing CMO destination at `/growth`.)
>
> **THE LOOP — `Read → Prove → Build`.** How you cross from Idea into Make. Owned by [application/realization/](./api/src/application/realization/), which declares **eight proof forms** — `demo-video`, `clickable-prototype`, `smoke-test`, `wizard-of-oz`, `poc`, `pilot`, `phone-line`, `live-system`. A ninth is a registry entry, never a branch.
>
> **Read and Prove spend nothing; only Build spends run budget.** **Every proof carries a kill condition**, graded in Measure, which closes the loop back to Idea. A proof with no condition that could fail is a launch with extra steps.
>
> A roadmap item earns its place by moving a real user through that loop. Enabling work (runtime, gateway, schema) is legitimate and marked as such — it does not outrank a broken Idea→Real journey.

## The Buyer — **the founder, then the team they hire**

> The North Star says what the product does. This says who it is done FOR, and it is the tie-breaker between two entries of equal size. It changes prioritization and public copy; it changes neither the method nor the seats.

**First buyer: a founder preparing to raise inside the next few months.** Solo, or a handful of people. They are the buyer the arc already fits — `Idea → Make → Run → Measure` is their COMPANY's arc, not just a project's — and they are the buyer the seat model was built for. A solo founder does not have a CFO; they have the CFO seat, and `DOMAIN_MANIFEST` already ships all seventeen.

**The hook is funding.** The flagship artifact is the fundraising pack — deck, model, metrics and data room, grounded on connected books rather than retyped. Most of it exists under a pre-sales label: [`rfpService.ts`](./api/src/application/rfp/rfpService.ts) already does freshness-gated portfolio matching, an LLM-spend-grounded P&L and a co-branded self-contained document, and [`dataRoomSharing.ts`](./api/src/application/investor/dataRoomSharing.ts) already does NDA gating, watermarking, expiry and per-file analytics.

**The retention mechanic is the diligence gap.** A required document sitting at `requested` in `due_diligence_documents` is a hole in the raise, and its `category` (`financial` · `legal` · `technical` · `commercial` · `people`) names the seat that closes it. The founder meets the second, third and fourth seat because an investor asked a question, not because a menu offered a feature.

**The expansion is the seat becoming a person.** They invite the human who fills the seat, and the workspace stops being one seat. **Pricing does not change** (operator decision 2026-08-24). The open question is packaging, not price: `PlanLimits.ts` caps Pro at `maxSeats: 1`, so the first teammate is a jump from $29/mo to a five-seat Teams minimum — a cliff at exactly the moment the product is working. `minimumSeats` is a field on a draft/publish pricing document, not a deploy.

**What this does not license.** Everything here is a DIRECTION, not a claim. Public copy about it is bound by the [Claim-to-Proof gate](#claim-to-proof-product-program) like any other promise — the pack is `🔄 Partial` until a release-gated journey test drives idea → connected books → pack → shared room → graded outcome. Two facts that copy must not outrun: **no accounting adapter has run against live production data**, so "computed from your ledger" is Planned, not Available; and **no BurnRateOS source rows have moved**, so nothing may be described as migrated.

## Key Metrics

**North star:** percentage of ideas reaching a **graded proof** — a Build whose kill condition was measured, not merely a deliverable produced.

**Read → Prove:** time to a chosen proof form · share of ideas that Read before they Build. **Build:** proofs reaching a real reachable artifact · validation/pass rate · delivery success and retry rate. **Measure:** share of proofs whose kill condition was graded. **Collaboration:** sessions inviting a human or agent · group-chat participation · synthesis rate. **Compounding:** sessions resumed within 7/30 days · outputs reused as inputs. **Efficiency:** interventions per successful delivery · cost and latency per outcome. **Integrity:** share of meaningful actions with a correlated terminal outcome.

Stars, downloads, workflow counts, MCP connections and active-agent counts are operational diagnostics, not evidence of customer value, and do not lead prioritization.

Every metric is computed from the correlated outcome ledger over the same dimensions — session, project, tenant, platform. Platform aggregates enforce minimum cohort sizes and never expose another tenant's content. Contract: **[Creation outcome metrics](./docs/design/creation-canvas/OUTCOME-METRICS.md)**.

## Consolidated Gap Register — index by domain

- **A delta ticket whose change is merged by a PULL REQUEST is still never completed.** `tickets.from_delta` opens its ticket in `in_review`, and until now nothing anywhere moved it out — every ticket the service ever minted sat at 50% on the board permanently, while the tool description told the model it "completes automatically once merged and deployed". The half where the RUN ITSELF pushes to the base branch is now closed (`shipVerification.shippedToBaseBranch` → `completeShippedTickets` in `brainRunStore`, and the directive/docblock in `WorkDeltaService` now describe only what is real). The remaining half is the PR path: a delta recorded in one run whose change is merged later by a pull request that run did not make. `githubWebhookRoutes` already receives `pull_request` with `merged: true` but only ingests it as activity — it does not resolve which `work_deltas` row the merge closes (the ledger records `files[]`/`projectId` but no branch or commit sha to join on). *Blocker: verifying this needs a live GitHub webhook delivery against a real merged PR — the join key has to be chosen against actual payloads, and a wrong one silently completes the wrong ticket, which is worse than leaving it open.* Unblocks: delta tickets closing themselves on the PR flow too, and retiring the backlog of stranded `in_review` tickets already on the board.

- **`pnpm dev` (Turbopack) cannot resolve pnpm-symlinked deps on Windows** — `next dev --turbopack` fails (`windows imports are not implemented yet`) and serves a 500 for every route; webpack dev (`npx next dev`) compiles the same tree fine, so the workaround is real but undiscoverable. *Blocker: an upstream Turbopack limitation on Windows UNC/symlink paths — needs an upstream fix or a decision to drop `--turbopack`.* Unblocks: local dev and browser-level debugging on Windows.

- **What RE-OPENS a role slot so the same ticket is dispatched hundreds of times is still unidentified — the damage is now bounded, the cause is not.** 766 tickets took 510,632 role dispatches between 2026-07-14 and 2026-08-04 (#147: 647 runs, 624 of them COMPLETED, ticket later `done`; #142: 769 runs; #674: 655 runs with 639 completions), and the dispatch counts map ~1:1 to real `executions`, so these were genuine cloud runs. The **`MAX_AUTONOMOUS_RUNS_PER_TASK` ceiling now caps this at 50 runs per ticket** at the one dispatch choke point, so no future loop can spend more than that without a human "Run now" — but the ticket still gets re-picked up to the ceiling, and the mechanism is unknown. Ruled OUT by dates: the `markRoleInProgress` marker and the breaker/cooldown at `dispatchCloudRunForTask` both landed 2026-07-26, and `recomputeParticipants`' `in_progress` preservation landed 2026-07-25 — the flood ran through all three until 2026-08-04. Remaining suspects are the re-open paths in `ticketParticipants.recomputeParticipants` (a `delegated` sign-off resetting a slot to `assigned`; a child task in a non-terminal status forcing `in_progress`). *Blocker: the subsystem has been fully idle since 2026-08-04 (0 dispatches, 0 executions, `ticket_participants.in_progress` frozen at that date), so the loop cannot be reproduced and a candidate fix cannot be verified — needs one live role-gated ticket run end-to-end, with the slot state sampled per sweep tick, to see which path re-opens it.* Unblocks: deleting the 510k residue rows (~280 MB, 80% of the transactional DB) with confidence, and removing the ceiling's reliance on being a backstop rather than a fix.

- **4 WebDiT models (cogvideox-2b/wan2.5/mochi-1/ltx2-distilled) are wired end-to-end in `studio`'s engine (types, MODEL_REGISTRY, `webdit-engine.ts`, `VideoEngine` dispatch) but registered `available: false`** — no pretrained bundle has been exported+uploaded to R2 yet. *Blocker: needs an external Python/diffusers→ONNX export per architecture (outside this repo) plus uploading the resulting multi-GB bundle to `studio-weights/<model>/` on R2.* Unblocks: flipping `available: true` and shipping the 4 new models.

- **`next build` fails to compile in this checkout: `@webdit/torch` cannot be resolved.** `webdit/runtime/package.json` correctly declares `"@webdit/torch": "file:../torch"`, but `webdit/runtime/node_modules` has no `@webdit` scope directory at all — the `file:` link was never installed for this checkout, so webpack's resolution of `voiceEngine.ts` → `voiceStudio.ts` → `@seanhogg/builderforce-studio` (`link:../studio`) → `@webdit/runtime` dies three levels down. Discovered while verifying an unrelated dashboard/founder's-journey pass (2026-08-26) — the import trace never touches a file that pass changed, and the repo has no root `package.json`/workspace tool that would auto-link `file:`/`link:` dependencies across `frontend`/`studio`/`webdit/*` on install, so this is an environment/install-step gap, not a code regression. *Blocker: needs someone who owns the webdit/studio linking to confirm the correct install sequence across the four `webdit/*` packages and re-run it* — reinstalling blind risked breaking the webdit↔studio integration that DONE.md records as landing the day before this was found. Unblocks: a real `next build` signal in CI/local dev; until then, typecheck + the full guard suite + touched test files are the verification surface for changes here.

- **Business Phone is a Twilio-backed virtual number (CPaaS), not an MVNO — a tenant cannot bring their existing mobile number in and use the platform as their carrier.** `phoneNumbers.ts`/`purchaseNumber` only issues NEW numbers Twilio owns; there is no port-in (LNP) flow to accept a customer's existing number, no SIM/eSIM issuance, and no wholesale/MVNE relationship with an underlying mobile network — none of which exists anywhere in this repo (confirmed by search). > Domain `agents` (seat: Platform). Everything that RUNS a model: cloud and on-prem runtimes, the PR loop, the LLM gateway and its routing, Evermind/SSM, and the Brain chat surface. Billing consequences are under [Finance](#-finance--billing-payouts--entitlements).

### Cloud agent execution & PR loop

- **Brain-created project has no repo → board-dispatched cloud coder degrades to reasoning-only.** `runCodingDispatch` (`agent-runtime/src/infra/builderforce-coding-dispatch.ts:132-140`) hits its "No repo bound" branch, so the coder returns prose. The single-prompt→app demo sidesteps it via the IDE (R2 + WebContainer); board dispatch still degrades. Fix = auto-provision a scratch repo at project-create, or route designer-modality projects through the IDE workspace.
- **Cloud agents wired but only happy-path validated — 50-gap PRD** ([09-prd-cloud-agent-validation.md](./specs/builderforce/09-prd-cloud-agent-validation.md)). P0: cloud V2 runs `bypassPermissions` + Bash on shared infra with no documented sandbox (G1); the Worker fallback honours no steering/cancel (S5/S6); errored/cancelled runs leak the task workspace (W2); a missing BYO key may silently fall to a platform-billed key (B2). O1/O2 (run reconstruction, ledger==snapshot) now have a home in the `qa:cloud-agents` script; the P0 sandbox, steering and workspace-leak gaps are unchanged.
- **`cloud-container` executor demotes to durable — INFRA.** The real Container (`AgentContainerDO` + `[[containers]]` + `api/container/`) is built; needs a Containers-enabled Workers Paid account and a `wrangler deploy` that builds the image. Known bounds: `sleepAfter` 20m may stop a >20m run (add keep-alive); a short-lived clone token is handed to the container shell (consider a scoped git-proxy); tokened clone URLs are GitHub-only. `CloudRunnerDO` is built but DO/alarm behaviour is unverifiable locally. The container surface has no `ask_human`/pause-resume op.
- **The cloud loop can't run build/type-check/tests in-run (no shell).** `run_checks` is a real static validator, but a full TS check needs the whole program and a shell — inherent to the Worker. Stays with CI-on-PR + human review + the webhook CI→auto-fix loop, or routes to the Container/on-prem sandbox. No pre-`finish` self-review loop (stub detection has a static ceiling). V1 still auto-merges unverified code when `CLOUD_AUTOMERGE_REQUIRE_GREEN` is off — make the green gate the default once webhooks are provisioned everywhere.
- **Cloud execution doesn't clone/analyze the repo or write `PRD.md` into it.** `runCloudExecution` is PRD-first and rules-aware but has no FS/subprocess. Needs a connected runtime. Hoist the PRD-first step into `dispatchAndQueue` so the flow is uniform for all engines. Downstream agents get the PRD read-only; PRD commit-to-branch is GitHub-only. No agent-facing task-mutation tool or agent-user identity; the assigned-agent ref reaches the card unresolved; Auto runs carry no `cloudAgentRef`.
- **Confirm the rest of the `never_started` cohort drains now that runs are graded.** Both structural causes are fixed and measured — the candidate window is no longer fixed (0384) and an unproductive run arms the autonomy breaker (0385) — and the cohort fell **370 → 200 (−46%)**. The remaining 200 may be refused for a reason `classifyBulkAutoRunReason` does not model. **Blocked on post-deploy data:** query `auto_run_skip` for five of the cohort's task ids and read `reason`. Unblocks: knowing whether the census tells the truth about why a ticket is not running.
- **Multi-repo spanning** — a run targets exactly ONE repo (one branch → one PR). A task crossing repos needs a per-task repo *set* with a branch+PR per repo and a loop routing each write via matchHints/pathGlobs.
- **Multi-provider PR/repo loop needs live validation.** GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket Cloud all cover create/merge/detail + read/write/PR. Residual: run request shapes against real GitLab/Bitbucket creds; Bitbucket detail is state/merged only; GitLab lacks per-file additions/deletions; Bitbucket code search is workspace-scoped + Premium-gated; both return a null `commitUrl`.
- **Post-merge build validation + auto-fix is GitHub-Actions-only**, reuses the merged ticket branch rather than a fresh `-fix-<n>`, only validates PRs with a recorded `merge_sha`, and gives the fix run step names rather than full log tails.
- **Cloud observability is poll-only.** Log lines derive from polled tool-audit behind a Refresh button; add a per-execution SSE/WS tail. Auto-run execution CREATION isn't pushed over the project socket, and container hard-death self-heal can't push a live event (needs a cross-isolate notifier). The relay log frame now takes an `executionId`, so relay streaming CAN be execution-scoped — what is missing is the tail itself.
- **LLM trace capture — one caller left.** `surface` labels cover gateway `chat`, `ide-chat`, `agent`, `dataset-gen`, and `BrainService` now threads a `traceId`. Remaining: **`/v1/images`** — `ImageProxyService` has no `logTrace` and is not a `ProxyResult`, so it needs a trace-shape adapter. Streamed traces capture tokens but not `response_body` (needs a stream tee); `ideAiRoutes.ts` streams aren't back-filled; cloud runs don't deep-link to their `llm_trace` row.
- **Capability guardrail + cross-board data isolation.** The guardrail only enforces an explicitly-set `required_capabilities` — no capability-aware router scoring task type vs agent skills, no default for unconfigured lanes — and board Triage surfaces a mismatch on demand only. `capabilities.load` telemetry conflates agent-pinned vs inherited artifacts. Moving a task across boards doesn't re-point `tasks.segment_id` to the target project's segment (isolation drift for segmented tenants).
- **The backlog lane seeds `gate:'auto'`, so staging work can spend tokens.** `templateCatalog.lane()` defaults every lane to `gate:'auto'`, and `backlog` holds 299 of the 313 `no_agent` tickets. Making it `gate:'human'` would stop a ticket parked for triage from being dispatched at all. **Blocker: an operator decision** — it changes the behaviour of every built-in template on live boards, and it is the same governance call as the `allowAutoStaffLanes` entry above. Unblocks: a backlog that costs nothing to hold.
- **Cloud auto-merge / finalize.** The Architect task is driven by `AnalysisRunnerDO`, not the tool loop, so it has no per-tool timeline/steering/approval; the worker-served project list omits `hasArchitecturePrd`; "View Arch Analysis" doesn't auto-open the drawer; there is no `POST /runs/:id/retry`; auto-run is manual-button only (no first-repo-map run, no commit-webhook re-analysis); generation uses `json_object` rather than strict `json_schema`.
- **The manager pass still carries the mechanical PR loop, and the fleet pool is a stopgap for a per-project Durable Object.** (1) The PR merge loop is mechanical, high-volume, provider-bound work that starved every stage behind it (93% of the pass). The merge queue bounds it to ~4s so it is no longer urgent, but the right shape is its own registry sweep alongside `runBoardSyncSweep` with its own budget, so the pass cannot be starved *by construction*. (2) `MANAGER_SWEEP_BUDGET_MS = 60_000` is a guess at the Worker's eviction point; the pool gives ~14 projects a tick at 25s/pass. If `notReached` stays near `projects`, the answer is a pass-per-project DO with its own alarm (the `ProjectEvermindCoordinatorDO` pattern), not a bigger pool. **Not blocked** — sequenced behind one capture of `notReached`.
- **The 309-ticket unconfigured-lane cohort has a name and a switch; nobody has thrown it.** `backlog` (299) and `blocked` (10) authorise no role, and `lane_unconfigured` + `allowAutoStaffLanes` (0386) stopped them masquerading as a staffing failure. What remains is the operator action: declare a required role on those stages, staff them by hand, or grant `allowAutoStaffLanes`. **Blocker: a governance decision, nothing technical** — the 429 capacity/routing fix landed 2026-08-19, so granting the switch no longer starts 309 tickets into a pool that would reject them. Unblocks: 46% of the stalled board.
- **One ticket carries three `pull_requests` rows for the same PR number.** `uq_pull_requests_provider_number` covers `(tenant_id, repo_id, number)` only `where repo_id is not null and number is not null`, so triplicates are possible in three shapes needing opposite fixes: NULL `repo_id`, genuinely different repos sharing a number, or rows predating the index. Every insert path audited passes a real `repoId`, so no current writer produces shape one. **Blocked on one query against the live database** (`select id, repo_id, number, task_id, created_at from pull_requests where task_id = <ticket>`) — it decides between backfilling + widening the index and closing this as a multi-repo misread. Unblocks: knowing whether the PR inventory has a uniqueness hole or a reporting one.
- **Milestone/dispatch chat messages render as plain assistant bubbles.** They carry `metadata.runMilestone`/`metadata.agentDispatch` but both renderers (web `BrainPanel`, VSIX webview) show them as ordinary emoji-prefixed text. Styling them as a system/activity line needs all five catalogs on both surfaces, and the milestone TEXT is server-generated English — localizing it needs a locale-aware template or client-side rendering off the structured metadata. Non-blocking.

### LLM gateway, routing & cost

- **`SUPERSEDED_MODEL_IDS` is hand-maintained — no drift check against live vendor `/models`.** `canonicalModelId` rewrites a stale pin at every seam a stored id enters dispatch, but the map is a hardcoded constant: the next model bump needs a human, and nothing fails CI when a vendor retires an id we still name. Fix = extend the live-`/models` reconciliation below to assert every KEY is still served or already mapped and every VALUE is present in the live payload. Unblocks: a vendor deprecation becoming a red build rather than a stalled ticket.
- **A known-broken BYO provider still LEADS the seed and is retried on every request.** `providerAuthAlerts` knows per tenant+provider that an account is rejected/unentitled/out-of-budget, but nothing feeds that verdict into `byoAutoSeedModels` — so a workspace with two providers leads with the one we know returned 401, burns an attempt, then fails over. `loadDemotedVendors` has exactly this shape for 5xx streaks but is keyed by vendor alone. Fix = resolve the alerted-vendor set where `tenantId` is in scope and pass it as `demotedVendors` — demote, never remove, since a `capacity` alert self-heals. **Blocked on a dependency-graph call:** the natural home (`resolveTenantLlmCredentials`) would import `providerAuthAlerts`, which imports it back. Unblocks: a broken account costing one wasted attempt total.
- **Model-catalog ids are hand-maintained and drift from live vendor `/models`.** Verify OpenRouter / Cloudflare `@cf/*` / NVIDIA NIM (incl. the `minimaxai/minimax-m2.7` default and `z-ai/glm-5.1`) / Google AI ids against each vendor's live endpoint — wrong ids 404 and pollute the cascade. Derive the FREE/PRO/CODING pools from the cached live payload (filter `supported_parameters: tools` + coding tag) so a stale id can't route to a 404 backstop. NVIDIA NIM TTS/audio and FluxAPI async-poll variants are unimplemented.
- **Cost caps / overflow.** Confirm the paid-overflow per-tenant cap + 60m cooldown gate free-plan cascades into `PAID_OVERFLOW_MODELS`, or fence the funded floor off entirely. No per-tenant cap on the always-on premium fallback for FREE quality-critical traffic (`useCase` is caller-supplied → an uncapped abuse vector). Pro is capped uniformly at 2 attempts with no plan-aware carve-out. No per-user sub-ledger from `metadata.userId`. Gateway chat/image calls and on-prem host runs are cost-unattributed; no workspace(segment) rollup or project spend widget.
- **Prompt / response caching.** Gateway and agent-runtime prompt-cache TTL is fixed 5-min ephemeral — wire `_builderforce.cacheTtl:'1h'` through the agent-runtime direct-Anthropic path. Explicit prompt caching is Anthropic-only. `CachingBridge`/`ResponseCache` (semantic response cache) is unused by both gateway and agent-runtime — wire into the read path for idempotent/low-temp `useCase`s. The gateway semantic-cache L2 is a 200-entry KV brute-force — upgrade to Vectorize and bind `SEMANTIC_CACHE_KV`. Web `SemanticCache` L1 ships; L2 needs a gateway endpoint + tenant token.
- **Reliability / diagnostics.** `applyCooldowns` issues 2N parallel KV writes per failed cascade (collapse to one composite-key blob); `dispatchVendor` doesn't re-check cooldown before each attempt; streaming responses don't surface `failovers[]`; streaming dispatch doesn't detect empty-but-200. The production gateway can drift stale (mixed npm/pnpm lockfiles in `api/`). Strict-mode validation only fires when a consumer sends `response_format: json_schema strict`, and per-vendor strict-schema capability is not advertised in `/v1/models`. `scoreWithLenientLLMOrFallback`/`LLM_UNAVAILABLE` lives outside this repo. The hired.video tailor still has its own client-side cascade and a stale model pin.
- **Tenant-model sampling `params`** apply at the gateway path only, not inside the V2 cloud agent loop (`AgentRunInput` carries no gen params). No persona picker in the My-LLMs editor though `tenant_models.persona_id` is server-supported. **Decision needed:** retire `tenant_model` presets in favour of published LLM Studio models, or rename the group.
- **Gateway hardening:** no message-shape sanitizer for strict vendors (a non-user-leading history or an empty tool-call `arguments` still trips Gemini `INVALID_ARGUMENT`); `llm` workflow nodes call the gateway with the host key, which may not be gateway-authorized.
- **Inheriting builds can still POST mutations to an Evermind they do not own.** The console renders read-only for an inherited Evermind (the real defect: the read path resolved the container's head so writes posted to the raw project id, updated zero rows and returned OK). The affordance is hidden but the endpoints still accept those writes; rejecting them with a 409 would change the agent/on-prem front-door contract. Unblocks: a write path that refuses honestly rather than no-oping.
- **Codex 403 stands the VENDOR down, not the RUN.** A 401/403 raises a retryable error tagged `CODEX_AUTH_MARKER`, so `classifyFailure`'s `auth` class applies a 30-minute single-strike cooldown and the cascade advances — deliberately not `VendorFatalError`, which would kill a servable request because one BYO account lost entitlement. Unblocks: an explicit decision on whether one dead BYO account should fail a request.
- **Vendor-health clear on the non-streaming success path is fire-and-forget.** `successJsonResult` is synchronous, so a recovered vendor can keep its demotion until the 30-minute TTL expires. The streaming path awaits the clear. Unblocks: immediate promotion on recovery.
- **No `web_search` adapter has run against its real live API.** All five (Tavily, Exa, Linkup, SearXNG, Wikipedia) are tested against a stubbed `fetch` — request shape, per-vendor mapping, private-URL dropping, and the 401/403/429/timeout/non-JSON contracts. **Blocked on credentials/infrastructure:** the keyed three need an account each and SearXNG needs a running instance. `/api/integrations/:id/test` runs a real query through the *same* adapter, so an operator's first "Test connection" is the genuine proof for the keyed three. Highest-risk assumption is per-vendor response field names (Tavily `content`, Exa `text`, Linkup `name`/`content`) — a wrong guess yields empty snippets rather than an error.
- **🌙 Kimi Code — a tenant with NO connected runtime still cannot use it from hosted cloud agents.** Kimi works today for any tenant running a Builderforce runtime (calls go over the relay from the user's own machine). Kimi's edge refuses Cloudflare Workers egress with an HTML 403 before the API reads the key; the byte-identical request returns a clean JSON 401 from an ordinary IP. The only remaining options are a static-egress relay (a hosted reverse proxy serving a subscription key — real ToS risk) or delegated auth. Spoofing a first-party client identity stays ruled out. **⚠️ Blocker: an approval decision from Kimi** on `docs/partnerships/kimi-code-hosted-integration-request.md`.

### Evermind / SSM

> Remaining items are ALL blocked (⛔) on a WebGPU runtime, GPU numerical validation, an operator credential, an external checkpoint, an upstream library, a product decision, or a live surface — except where marked otherwise.

- ⛔ **P0 — real backprop in the trainer.** `MambaTrainer._trainStep` feeds only `dLogits` to AdamW for every param — no backward pass runs (the autograd tape and scan/ssd backward kernels exist but are unused). `learn()` is SAFE (WSLA freezes the backbone, trust region + NaN guard, rollback-on-regression) but ineffective. *Blocker: a WebGPU runtime to validate numerically.*
- ⛔ **P1 — surface-agnostic run-context + embedded-Evermind delta wiring.** Context is assembled 3× (cloud `prepareCloudRun`, on-prem `buildEmbeddedSystemPrompt`, VS Code `prompt.ts`); on-prem and VS Code runs are starved of strategic/PRD/governance context (`TaskAssignMessage` carries only `{task, artifact-slugs}`); no surface injects board settings, project vision, the epic→OKR chain or `tasks.priority`; nothing routes context through `EvermindCognition` for delta/reconciliation (cloud re-sends the full PRD + governance + repo listing every run); no surface writes strategic state back. Fix = ONE shared `ContextSource` (keyed blocks, version tokens, read-through cache) + `ContextReconciler`, with the three builders becoming thin `assembleAgentContext` consumers. Also subsumes capability-loader parity. *Blocker: an execution-critical rewrite of all three prompt-assembly surfaces — each surface RUN must validate.*
- ⛔ **P2 — grad-norm clip under-counts across >1 workgroup** (`weight_update.ts` non-atomic `norm_sq[0]` RMW race). Low impact today (the trust region bounds every step); wrong for large-model training. *Blocker: GPU numerical validation.*
- ⛔ **P2 — foreign-checkpoint weight-port mapping.** The importer round-trips Evermind's own exports and warm-starts via a caller-supplied `rename` map, but real Codestral-Mamba/Falcon-Mamba ports need tensor names mapped plus scan-vs-depthwise-conv mixer reconciliation. Transformer coders can't be weight-ported at all — distill only. *Blocker: the real external checkpoints.*
- ⛔ **P2 — learned-routing on-prem/IDE consumer.** The write-back door ships in api (`POST /llm/v1/run-outcome` + mig 0283). Open: the on-prem Node host should classify action type, read cached `routing:<scope>`, compute a local SSM bias, seed via `rankModelsForAction`, and call `/v1/run-outcome` at completion — grep confirms zero of these in `agent-runtime`. *Blocker: re-ranking changes model selection on real runs — needs a running host.*
- ⛔ **P2 — Evermind auto-routing eval gate.** Evermind is pin-only (`autoRoute:false`); nothing promotes a qualifying `evermind/<ref>` into the auto-route ranking. *Blocker: a product decision on gate criteria (use cases, benchmark threshold, opt-in scope) plus live validation — it routes billed traffic.*
- ⛔ **P3 — on-prem adaptation cost unbenchmarked** (tune window/epoch/skip-threshold under load — needs a live host); **stdio MCP recall is lexical (Jaccard), not SSM-embedding** (the headless binary doesn't stand up the GPU runtime).
- ⛔ **Blocked externally:** transformers.js `pipeline()` auto-registration for `model_type:"evermind"`; llama.cpp GGUF `architecture:"evermind"` execution; a live Hugging Face push (needs a write-scoped `HF_TOKEN`). The `.evermind` package doesn't embed its tokenizer — a portability nicety, not blocking.
- **P3 (not blocked) — Prompt API per-turn system-prompt fidelity.** `PromptApiModelProvider` fixes the system prompt at session creation, so the per-step `systemPrompt` assembled in `AgentRuntime.step` never reaches Chrome's built-in model — only its per-turn `memoryContext`/`fileContext` do. Fix = detect a changed system prompt and `session.clone()`/recreate, or fold the delta into the turn input. *Needs a Chrome build with the Prompt API to validate end-to-end.*

#### Evermind — enterprise layer, distillation, tool calling & memory residuals

- ⛔ **The three vendor dialects and the Vertex bridge have never spoken to a live service.** `qdrant`, `pinecone` and `vertex-ai` (plus `VertexAIBridge`/`VertexAIEmbedder`) are covered only by unit tests asserting the emitted body and parsing a recorded response — which catches a wrong field name but not a wrong endpoint version, a changed auth scheme, or an undocumented pagination rule. The `evermind` dialect is exempt: this repo defines that contract. *Blocker: no Qdrant/Pinecone/GCP credentials exist in this checkout.* Unblocks: quoting a customer's existing vector database as supported rather than "adapter written, unproven".
- **P2 (not blocked) — two lexical scorers with different tokenizers.** `api/src/domain/webSearch/textIndex.ts` implements its own stopword+stemming tokenizer and scoring, while `builderforce-memory`'s `bm25Search` uses the package's plain `tokenize`. Not straight duplicates — the api's stemming is a genuine specialization — but two answers to "how do we score lexical relevance", and they will drift. Fix = give `Bm25Options` an optional `tokenize` hook, then have `textIndex` supply its stemmer and delegate. Not done in this pass because the hook with no consumer is a dead seam, and wiring it needs a web-search regression run to prove ranking unchanged.
- **P2 — the platform api consumes the retrieval primitives but not the new ports.** `agentKnowledge.ts`/`recallSops.ts` import `hybridRetrieve`/`bm25Search`, but nothing in `api/` uses `VectorStore`, `IngestionPipeline`, `EnterpriseRetriever`, `AgentGraph`, `Tracer`/`MetricsRegistry` or `EvalHarness`. Concretely: knowledge ingestion is bespoke per surface with no content-hash incremental sync (a re-index re-embeds everything), agent-knowledge scoping is enforced in application code rather than compiled into the store query, and there is no per-request cost/TTFT attribution on the gateway. Adoption is a distinct program — each is a migration with its own tenant-data blast radius. Unblocks: the enterprise story being true of the PRODUCT, not only the library.
- **P3 — `Tracer` has no head-based propagation helper.** A trace continued across an HTTP hop must have its `traceId` passed in; nothing parses or emits a W3C `traceparent`. Fine for one process, insufficient once portal → api → relay → runtime all want one trace. Fix = a `traceparent` parse/format pair plus the sampling flag. Unblocks: one span tree across the four hops of a cloud run.
- ⛔ **`POST /api/projects/:id/evermind/learn` (the pre-diffed weight-delta door) has no caller anywhere.** Every real learning path goes through `/evermind/learn-text` and fits server-side in `ProjectEvermindCoordinatorDO.drain()`, so `kind: 'delta'` contributions never occur and the stale-`baseVersion` guard is untested. *Blocker: a product decision (wire the on-prem runner to push diffs vs retire the door) plus a live coordinator DO.*
- ⛔ **Legacy distillation ring entries carry no provenance.** Entries merged before the provenance fields existed have no `distilled`/`skipReason`, and DO ring rows are never rewritten. `evermindLearnedStatus` infers the one provable case, but a legacy refine-mode entry whose teacher silently failed still renders as `self`. *Blocker: a per-DO storage migration walking `RECENT_KEY` on a live runtime.*
- ⛔ **The teach action reports success before the teacher has run.** `/evermind/learn-text` enqueues and returns 200; the frontier call happens later in the debounced merge alarm, so the console toasts "Taught" even when distillation subsequently faults (discoverable in the Learnings ring, and a broken teacher now raises a `/quality` alert). *Blocker: point-of-teach feedback needs a post-merge notification or a pollable per-contribution status channel.*
- ⛔ **"Import from builderforce-memory" — live-store clobber.** Compaction rewrites the snapshot, but a RUNNING MCP stdio server holds the store in memory and re-snapshots over the stubs on its next `remember`/`forget`, so stubs take effect only after respawn. Fix: have the server watch its snapshot for external compaction, or expose a first-class `memory_compact` tool. *Blocker: proving the race closes needs a live stdio server + MCP client.*
- ⛔ **Markdown auto-memory not importable.** The importer targets the JSON snapshot only. A directory/frontmatter parser is trivial, but the compaction protocol assumes ONE file path — a directory of per-fact files means threading per-file paths through `pickMemory` → `extract-memories` → `compactMemory` and per-file stub rewriting. *Blocker: verifiable only against a live VS Code extension host.*
- **P3 — the diagnostics report body is deliberately English-only.** `diagnosticsReport.ts` emits an unlocalized markdown document (the buttons around it ARE localized) — the right call for a technical artifact in the same category as a stack trace. Recorded so the choice is not mistaken for an oversight.
- **P2 — a tool-calling turn costs one forward pass per candidate and per scored argument.** `createEvermindToolDecoder` has no incremental/KV state, and the published engine's `forward()` recomputes the full sequence — so a loop offering ~20 tools pays ~20 passes for the tool vote alone. Mitigated only by capping the prompt at `MAX_TOOL_PROMPT_CHARS` (6000). Fix: an engine-side prefix cache so candidates share the prompt pass, or one batched scoring pass. Unblocks: pinning Evermind on agent loops with a full tool catalogue.
- **P3 — reconcile is a lexical-overlap heuristic, not a true supersede check.** `countReconciledMemories` counts a recalled memory as reconciled when the answer restates ≥60% of its meaningful tokens; the real write-through supersede happens weight-side in the coordinator merge. ## 🧲 Hiring — postings, sourcing & applications

Unblocks: the "review a seat on its own" property the root-table guard protects, extended past the root to the family beneath it.

### Frontend architecture, i18n & admin

- **COMPONENT-level code splitting is rare — `next/dynamic` in 9 files, zero `React.lazy`** *(PRD 22 re-audit)*. Note the qualifier: **library-level splitting is established and is not a gap** — Mermaid, xterm + 2 addons, WebContainer, onnxruntime-web and y-monaco are all dynamically imported, 57 `await import()` sites in total. The gap is that a runtime `import()` inside an already-loaded client module defers a *library*, whereas `next/dynamic`/`React.lazy` defers a *component subtree* including everything it statically imports — which is what PRD 22 §3.1 requires, and that exists in only 4 files. Fix = scope PRD 22 Phase 1 as *introduction of the component-level pattern* with a per-route owner list (training, 3D, voice, game, Studio panels), sequenced after the RSC conversion so the gain is attributable.
- **i18n — only a handful of surfaces are migrated** (`/settings` is the reference); ~330 components still hold hardcoded English. Biggest un-migrated: marketing copy in `content.ts` + `/evermind` + `PricingPageClient` + blog markdown; `QaContent.tsx`; `AgentPublishPanel`; `TaskMgmtContent`; `dashboard/page.tsx` + `ProjectsContent`; `VoiceClonePanel`; the whole Sidebar/nav chrome; `OnboardingStepper`; `CloudAgentFormFields`; `IntegrationCredentialsManager`/`BoardConnectionsManager`. VS Code runtime l10n bundles (de/es/fr/zh) for `projectsTree`/page labels. No CJK font coverage (add a `system-ui` CJK fallback stack); public pages render dynamically (split a `/[locale]/` segment for the ~7 public pages if SEO/cache matters); the client payload is not namespace-scoped; there is no machine-translation pipeline. **Standing rule:** any page/component ADDED or MODIFIED must be localized in all five catalogs, dark+light and mobile-friendly in the same pass — see [[i18n-localization]], [[theme-and-responsive-ui]].
- **The message catalogs are partitioned by LOCALE, which is a presentation axis, so no bounded context owns its own ubiquitous language.** `messages/{en,zh,es,fr,de}.json` is five files of ~892 KB holding **243 top-level namespaces**, so the FIRST partition of the translation store is *language* — a rendering concern, like theme. That is why the architecture rule's own words do not hold here: "ubiquitous language shared by schema/API/UI" means a context's words are PART of that context, and today sixteen domains' vocabulary sits in one bucket keyed by locale with no owner per key. The merge conflict every parallel agent hits is the symptom; the missing ownership is the defect. **The runtime is not the reason, and that settles it:** `publish-message-catalogs.mjs` already emits a per-locale asset into `public/i18n/` and `i18n/catalog.ts` fetches exactly the one a request needs — so the per-locale bundle is **already a projection**, built precisely because importing 4.7 MB broke the 4 MB Edge ceiling ([[i18n-catalogs-are-assets]]). A projection is shaped for its reader; the SOURCE must be shaped for its owner. Fix = partition the source by bounded context, one directory per domain, **isomorphic to `schema/<domain>.ts`** — `messages/<domain>/{en,zh,es,fr,de}.json`, ~16 directories rather than 243 loose keys — with namespaces re-keyed under their owning domain (`commerce.stage.*`, `growth.site.*`, `canvas.app.*`), the publish script folding domain files into the same per-locale asset it emits today (**no runtime change, no new fetch**), and `messages.test.ts` walking directories instead of importing five files. Contested namespaces go to `common/` under a **shrink-only ratchet**, never a bucket that quietly stops shrinking. **Not blocked** — one mechanical rewrite of 4.7 MB of JSON plus a publish-script and test change, landing as ONE commit because a half-partitioned store has two answers to "where are the messages".
- **P3 — Auth-page marketing copy sourced from `content.ts` is English-only.** The Register/Login form bodies are localized, but their marketing panels pull static English from `frontend/src/lib/content.ts` (the login intro, `LOGIN_FAQ`, every `REGISTER_MARKETING` variant) — a shared 1,591-line module consumed across the public site. Fix = migrate its marketing strings to next-intl catalogs.
- **P3 — No sweep for pre-existing fake/unverified accounts.** Migration 0285 backfills `email_verified_at = created_at` for EVERY existing account, so fake accounts created before the OTP gate remain `verified`; the gate only stops NEW fake signups. Fix (optional ops task) = a one-time admin sweep flagging never-signed-in / disposable-domain accounts.
- **P3 — Ceremony drag-and-drop is non-functional on touch (mobile is view-only).** The stage's assign/group/schedule/return-to-backlog interactions rely on HTML5 drag-and-drop, which does not fire on touch. The mobile-responsive pass makes the stage READABLE on a phone but a mobile user still cannot assign work by dragging. Fix = a tap-to-assign fallback or a pointer-based DnD lib.

### Infrastructure, CI/CD & cost

- **🛡️ Cloudflare serves a managed challenge to every datacenter caller of the whole `builderforce.ai` zone.** Any request from an automated/datacenter IP gets a `403` + `cf-mitigated: challenge` ("Just a moment…" HTML) instead of reaching the worker, on EVERY hostname. Measured from a GitHub runner against `POST api.builderforce.ai/mcp`, `GET /health`, `POST builderforce.ai/gateway/mcp`, `GET builderforce.ai/` and `GET www.builderforce.ai/` — all five returned it, steady across 5 attempts, unchanged by a browser `User-Agent`, identical over IPv4. The same requests answer correctly from a residential IP, so the worker is healthy and the mitigation is at the edge. **This is not only a CI problem:** the audience of the MCP Registry listing (Anthropic connectors, AWS, Gemini Enterprise, hosted agents) is exactly the class of caller being challenged, and `builderforce.ai/gateway/*` — documented as "the ONE whitelisted host all clients should target" — is challenged too. The `publish-mcp-registry` job refuses to publish under this condition and since 2026-08-14 WARNS rather than failing the release on this specific path (it had painted every push to main red for a condition the workflow cannot clear, which is how a genuine `deploy-api`/`deploy-frontend` regression goes unnoticed); the publish is gated on the probe's `publish` output so "warn" cannot degrade into "publish anyway", and any OTHER unexpected status still fails hard. **FOUR settings emit this same header, with different remedies:** Bot Fight Mode (free plan) runs outside the Ruleset Engine and accepts **no** exceptions — Skip/Bypass/Allow "have no effect" per Cloudflare's docs — so it can only be turned OFF; Super Bot Fight Mode (Pro+) *is* skippable, but only by a rule naming the `http_request_sbfm` phase; Security Level `high`/`under_attack` challenges by IP reputation, which is what datacenter egress scores as; and Browser Integrity Check challenges anything whose headers aren't a browser, i.e. every correct API client. The last two are skippable as the `securityLevel` and `bic` products. `scripts/cloudflare-bot-exception.mjs` reads which mode is on and applies the matching remedy under `--apply`. **BLOCKER: the fix is a Cloudflare zone setting** — someone with the dashboard must flip the toggle or mint a token scoped to the zone with Zone Settings:Edit + WAF:Edit and run that script (`CF_API_TOKEN` is a Workers-deploy token, not zone WAF). ### 🏛️ Architecture pass — residuals

- **The layering ratchet is at 126 files and IS falling.** `npm run check:layering` stops NEW `presentation → infrastructure` imports; **110** non-test presentation modules still import the schema directly and hold **286 selects / 108 inserts / 84 updates** inline in the route layer. The baseline only shrinks when someone touching a route moves its queries into an application service. Worst offenders: `adminRoutes`, `agentHostRoutes`, `qaRoutes`, `knowledgeRoutes`. - **271 unscoped tenant queries remain baselined across 107 files.** Down from 524/168. **What is left is NOT the same kind of work as what was just done.** The 253 that converted did so because a tenant id was already in scope; the transformation was mechanical and `tsgo` proved each one. The compiler REJECTED 219 more, and every rejection means the same thing: **that function does not know its tenant.** Those are the documented "safe via an owner-checked parent" population, and making them self-scoping is not an edit — it is threading a tenant id down a call chain, changing signatures across a slice per chain. So the remaining 271 need per-chain design decisions (thread `tenantId`, or accept the parent check and record why), not another codemod. Unblocks: retiring the baseline, at which point the guard becomes a wall instead of a ratchet.
- **23 of 38 permissions are enforced; the remaining 15 are advisory for STRUCTURAL reasons, not for want of a middleware line.** The delivery surfaces operators want overrides on are real gates (`project:read/write/delete`, `task:read/write/delete/assign`, `workflow:read/write`, `member:read`, `report:read/export`).
